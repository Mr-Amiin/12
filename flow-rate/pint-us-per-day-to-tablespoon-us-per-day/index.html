About Converting Pint per Days to Tablespoon per Days

Pint per Days and Tablespoon per Days both measure volumetric flow rate — how much volume passes a point over time — used to size pipes and pumps, monitor river or stream discharge, control industrial processes, and set medical infusion rates. Both are volumetric flow rate units.

Formula

tablespoon per days = pint per days × 32

This factor comes from each unit's defined relationship to the category's base unit: 1 pint per days equals 5.47658e-9 base units, and 1 tablespoon per days equals 1.711431e-10 base units, so dividing one by the other gives the direct pint per days-to-tablespoon per days factor of 32.

Simple example

1 pt/d × 32 = 32 tbsp/d

1 pint per days = 32 tablespoon per days.

Real-world example

What's the difference between volumetric and mass flow rate?

Volumetric flow rate measures the volume of fluid passing a point per unit time (for example, liters per second). Mass flow rate measures the mass instead. For a fluid of constant density the two are directly proportional, but for compressible fluids like gases, mass flow is often the more meaningful quantity.
