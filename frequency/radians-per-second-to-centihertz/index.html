About Converting Radians per second to Centihertz

Radians per second and Centihertz both describe how fast something cycles or rotates, and the conversion is a routine part of reading a car tachometer or motor spec sheet against a scientific frequency figure. Specifically: Angular frequency converted by one revolution equals 2*pi radians, and centihertz is 0.01 hertz units.

Formula

centihertz = radians per second × 15.9154943092

This factor comes from each unit's defined relationship to the category's base unit: 1 radians per second equals 0.159154943092 base units, and 1 centihertz equals 0.01 base units, so dividing one by the other gives the direct radians per second-to-centihertz factor of 15.9154943092.
