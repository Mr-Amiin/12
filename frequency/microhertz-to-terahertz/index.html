About Converting Microhertz to Terahertz

Microhertz and Terahertz both describe how fast something cycles or rotates, and the conversion is a routine part of reading a car tachometer or motor spec sheet against a scientific frequency figure. Specifically: Microhertz is 0.000001 hertz units, and terahertz is 1000000000000 hertz units.

Formula

terahertz = microhertz × 1e-18

This factor comes from each unit's defined relationship to the category's base unit: 1 microhertz equals 0.000001 base units, and 1 terahertz equals 1e+12 base units, so dividing one by the other gives the direct microhertz-to-terahertz factor of 1e-18.

Simple example

1 uHz × 1e-18 = 1e-18 THz

1 microhertz = 1e-18 terahertz.

Real-world example

1,000 uHz × 1e-18 = 1e-15 THz

1,000 microhertz = 1e-15 terahertz.
