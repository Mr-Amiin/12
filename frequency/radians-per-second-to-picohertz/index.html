About Converting Radians per second to Picohertz

Radians per second and Picohertz both describe how fast something cycles or rotates, and the conversion is a routine part of reading a car tachometer or motor spec sheet against a scientific frequency figure. Specifically: Angular frequency converted by one revolution equals 2*pi radians, and picohertz is 1e-12 hertz units.

Formula

picohertz = radians per second × 159154943092

This factor comes from each unit's defined relationship to the category's base unit: 1 radians per second equals 0.159154943092 base units, and 1 picohertz equals 1e-12 base units, so dividing one by the other gives the direct radians per second-to-picohertz factor of 159154943092.
