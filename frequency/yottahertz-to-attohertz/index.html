About Converting Yottahertz to Attohertz

Formula

attohertz = yottahertz × 1e+42

This factor comes from each unit's defined relationship to the category's base unit: 1 yottahertz equals 1e+24 base units, and 1 attohertz equals 1e-18 base units, so dividing one by the other gives the direct yottahertz-to-attohertz factor of 1e+42.
