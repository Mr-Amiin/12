About Converting Petahertz to Terahertz

Petahertz and Terahertz both describe how fast something cycles or rotates, and the conversion is a routine part of reading a car tachometer or motor spec sheet against a scientific frequency figure. Specifically: Petahertz is 1000000000000000 hertz units, and terahertz is 1000000000000 hertz units.

Formula

terahertz = petahertz × 1000

This factor comes from each unit's defined relationship to the category's base unit: 1 petahertz equals 1e+15 base units, and 1 terahertz equals 1e+12 base units, so dividing one by the other gives the direct petahertz-to-terahertz factor of 1000.

Simple example

1 PHz × 1000 = 1,000 THz

1 petahertz = 1,000 terahertz.

Real-world example

1,000 PHz × 1000 = 1,000,000 THz

1,000 petahertz = 1,000,000 terahertz.
