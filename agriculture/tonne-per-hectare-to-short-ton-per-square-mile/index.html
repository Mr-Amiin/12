Understanding the Metric ton per Hectare

Metric ton per Hectare (t/ha) measures agricultural yield/application rate. One tonne (1,000 kg) of material per hectare (10,000 square meters) of land — the standard way crop yield is reported internationally. It is classified as a metric agricultural rate unit.

Where it's used: The standard international unit for crop yield reporting outside the US (FAO and most national agricultural statistics)

t/ha vs. kg/ha. The same quantity at different scales: 1 t/ha equals 1,000 kg/ha. t/ha is typically used for whole-field yield reporting, while kg/ha suits precise fertilizer or pesticide application rates.

Uses of the Metric ton per Hectare today

  • National and international crop yield statistics (FAO reporting)
  • Agronomy research and field-trial yield comparisons
  • Large-scale fertilizer and soil-amendment application-rate planning
