About Converting Kilograms per Hectare to Pounds per Square Kilometer

Kilogram per Hectare and Pound per Square Kilometer both measure a farming-related quantity — a land area or an application/yield rate per unit area — used to report crop yields, calculate how much fertilizer or seed a field needs, and plan irrigation. Kilogram per Hectare is most often used for fertilizer and pesticide application-rate labeling.

Formula

pounds per square kilometer = kilograms per hectare × 220.462262185

This factor comes from each unit's defined relationship to the category's base unit: 1 kilogram per hectare equals 0.0001 base units, and 1 pound per square kilometer equals 4.535924e-7 base units, so dividing one by the other gives the direct kilogram per hectare-to-pound per square kilometer factor of 220.462262185.

Understanding the Kilogram per Hectare

Kilogram per Hectare (kg/ha) measures agricultural application/yield rate. One kilogram of material per hectare (10,000 square meters) of land. It is classified as a metric agricultural rate unit.

Where it's used: The standard international unit for fertilizer, pesticide, and seed application rates outside the US

Uses of the Kilogram per Hectare today

  • Fertilizer and pesticide application-rate labeling
  • Crop yield reporting in most countries outside the US
  • Agronomy research and soil-nutrient planning

What's the difference between a yield rate and an application rate?

A yield rate describes how much of a crop is harvested per unit of land area (for example, tonnes per hectare). An application rate describes how much of an input like fertilizer or seed is applied per unit of land area. Both are commonly expressed using the same kind of mass-per-area units.
