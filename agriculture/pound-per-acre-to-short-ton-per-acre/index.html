About Converting Pounds per Acre to Short tons per Acre

Pound per Acre and Short ton per Acre both measure a farming-related quantity — a land area or an application/yield rate per unit area — used to report crop yields, calculate how much fertilizer or seed a field needs, and plan irrigation. Both are mass application rate units.

Formula

short tons per acre = pounds per acre × 0.0005

This factor comes from each unit's defined relationship to the category's base unit: 1 pound per acre equals 0.000112085115619 base units, and 1 short ton per acre equals 0.224170231239 base units, so dividing one by the other gives the direct pound per acre-to-short ton per acre factor of 0.0005.

Simple example

1 lb/ac × 0.0005 = 0.0005 ton/ac

1 pound per acre = 0.0005 short tons per acre.

Real-world example

100 lb/ac × 0.0005 = 0.05 ton/ac

100 pounds per acre = 0.05 short tons per acre.

Is the pound per acre to short ton per acre conversion exact?

Yes. Both pound per acre and short ton per acre are defined by fixed standards rather than physical artifacts, so the factor of 0.0005 used above is exact to as many digits as you choose to display.

What's the difference between a yield rate and an application rate?

A yield rate describes how much of a crop is harvested per unit of land area (for example, tonnes per hectare). An application rate describes how much of an input like fertilizer or seed is applied per unit of land area. Both are commonly expressed using the same kind of mass-per-area units.
