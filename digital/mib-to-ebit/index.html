About Converting MiBs to Exabits

MiB and Exabit are everyday digital-storage units, used for everything from file sizes and app downloads to storage device capacity and mobile data allowances. Specifically: MiB digital storage unit, and exabit digital storage unit.

Formula

exabits = mibs × 8.388608e-12

This factor comes from each unit's defined relationship to the category's base unit: 1 mib equals 8388608 base units, and 1 exabit equals 1e+18 base units, so dividing one by the other gives the direct mib-to-exabit factor of 8.388608e-12.

Simple example

1 MiB × 8.388608e-12 = 8.388608e-12 Eb

1 mib = 8.388608e-12 exabits.

Real-world example

500 MiB × 8.388608e-12 = 4.194304e-9 Eb

500 mibs = 4.194304e-9 exabits.
