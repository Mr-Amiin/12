About Converting PiBs to ZB

PiB and ZB are everyday digital-storage units, used for everything from file sizes and app downloads to storage device capacity and mobile data allowances. Specifically: PiB digital storage unit, and zB digital storage unit.

Formula

zb = pibs × 0.00000112589990684

This factor comes from each unit's defined relationship to the category's base unit: 1 pib equals 9.007199e+15 base units, and 1 zb equals 8e+21 base units, so dividing one by the other gives the direct pib-to-zb factor of 0.00000112589990684.
