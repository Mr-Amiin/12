About Converting YB to Bits

YB and Bit are everyday digital-storage units, used for everything from file sizes and app downloads to storage device capacity and mobile data allowances. Specifically: YB digital storage unit, and bit digital storage unit.

Formula

bits = yb × 8e+24

This factor comes from each unit's defined relationship to the category's base unit: 1 yb equals 8e+24 base units, and 1 bit equals 1 base unit, so dividing one by the other gives the direct yb-to-bit factor of 8e+24.

Simple example

1 YB × 8e+24 = 8e+24 bit

1 yb = 8e+24 bits.

Real-world example

500 YB × 8e+24 = 4e+27 bit

500 yb = 4e+27 bits.

Frequently asked questions

How many bits are in 1 yb?

1 yb equals 8e+24 bits, using the exact defined conversion factor rather than an estimate.

How do I convert yb to bit?

Multiply the yb value by 8e+24. The converter above does this instantly to whatever precision you set.

How do I convert bit back to yb?

Use the reverse factor: 1 bit equals 1.25e-25 yb. You can also use the swap control in the converter above to flip the direction instantly.

What is a yb?

YB (YB) is a unit of digital storage.

What is a bit?

Bit (bit) is a unit of digital storage.

Is the yb to bit conversion exact?

Yes. Both yb and bit are defined by fixed standards rather than physical artifacts, so the factor of 8e+24 used above is exact to as many digits as you choose to display.
