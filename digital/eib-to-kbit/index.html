About Converting EiBs to Kilobits

EiB and Kilobit are everyday digital-storage units, used for everything from file sizes and app downloads to storage device capacity and mobile data allowances. Specifically: EiB digital storage unit, and kilobit digital storage unit.

Formula

kilobits = eibs × 9.223372e+15

This factor comes from each unit's defined relationship to the category's base unit: 1 eib equals 9.223372e+18 base units, and 1 kilobit equals 1000 base units, so dividing one by the other gives the direct eib-to-kilobit factor of 9.223372e+15.

1 eib = 9.223372e+15 kilobits.
