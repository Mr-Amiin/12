About Converting TiBs to Bits

TiB and Bit are everyday digital-storage units, used for everything from file sizes and app downloads to storage device capacity and mobile data allowances. Specifically: TiB digital storage unit, and bit digital storage unit.

Formula

bits = tibs × 8.796093e+12

This factor comes from each unit's defined relationship to the category's base unit: 1 tib equals 8.796093e+12 base units, and 1 bit equals 1 base unit, so dividing one by the other gives the direct tib-to-bit factor of 8.796093e+12.

Simple example

1 TiB × 8.796093e+12 = 8.796093e+12 bit

1 tib = 8.796093e+12 bits.

Real-world example
