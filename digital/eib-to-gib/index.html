About Converting EiBs to GiBs

EiB and GiB are everyday digital-storage units, used for everything from file sizes and app downloads to storage device capacity and mobile data allowances. Specifically: EiB digital storage unit, and giB digital storage unit.

Formula

gibs = eibs × 1073741824

This factor comes from each unit's defined relationship to the category's base unit: 1 eib equals 9.223372e+18 base units, and 1 gib equals 8589934592 base units, so dividing one by the other gives the direct eib-to-gib factor of 1073741824.
