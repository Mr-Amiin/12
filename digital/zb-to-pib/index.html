About Converting ZB to PiBs

ZB and PiB are everyday digital-storage units, used for everything from file sizes and app downloads to storage device capacity and mobile data allowances. Specifically: ZB digital storage unit, and piB digital storage unit.

Formula

pibs = zb × 888178.4197

This factor comes from each unit's defined relationship to the category's base unit: 1 zb equals 8e+21 base units, and 1 pib equals 9.007199e+15 base units, so dividing one by the other gives the direct zb-to-pib factor of 888178.4197.
