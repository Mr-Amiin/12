About Converting PiBs to Petabits

PiB and Petabit are everyday digital-storage units, used for everything from file sizes and app downloads to storage device capacity and mobile data allowances. Specifically: PiB digital storage unit, and petabit digital storage unit.

Formula

petabits = pibs × 9.00719925474

This factor comes from each unit's defined relationship to the category's base unit: 1 pib equals 9.007199e+15 base units, and 1 petabit equals 1e+15 base units, so dividing one by the other gives the direct pib-to-petabit factor of 9.00719925474.

Simple example

1 PiB × 9.007199255 = 9.007199255 Pb

1 pib = 9.007199255 petabits.

Real-world example
