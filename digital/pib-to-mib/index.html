About Converting PiBs to MiBs

PiB and MiB are everyday digital-storage units, used for everything from file sizes and app downloads to storage device capacity and mobile data allowances. Specifically: PiB digital storage unit, and miB digital storage unit.

Formula

mibs = pibs × 1073741824

This factor comes from each unit's defined relationship to the category's base unit: 1 pib equals 9.007199e+15 base units, and 1 mib equals 8388608 base units, so dividing one by the other gives the direct pib-to-mib factor of 1073741824.

Simple example

1 PiB × 1073741824 = 1,073,741,824 MiB

1 pib = 1,073,741,824 mibs.

Real-world example

500 PiB × 1073741824 = 536,870,912,000 MiB

500 pibs = 536,870,912,000 mibs.
