About Converting Terabits to EiBs

Terabit and EiB are everyday digital-storage units, used for everything from file sizes and app downloads to storage device capacity and mobile data allowances. Specifically: Terabit digital storage unit, and eiB digital storage unit.

Formula

eibs = terabits × 1.084202e-7

This factor comes from each unit's defined relationship to the category's base unit: 1 terabit equals 1e+12 base units, and 1 eib equals 9.223372e+18 base units, so dividing one by the other gives the direct terabit-to-eib factor of 1.084202e-7.
