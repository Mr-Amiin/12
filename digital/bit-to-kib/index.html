About Converting Bits to KiBs

Bit and KiB are everyday digital-storage units, used for everything from file sizes and app downloads to storage device capacity and mobile data allowances. Specifically: Bit digital storage unit, and kiB digital storage unit.

Formula

kibs = bits × 0.0001220703125

This factor comes from each unit's defined relationship to the category's base unit: 1 bit equals 1 base unit, and 1 kib equals 8192 base units, so dividing one by the other gives the direct bit-to-kib factor of 0.0001220703125.

Simple example

1 bit × 0.0001220703125 = 0.0001220703 KiB

1 bit = 0.0001220703 kibs.

Real-world example

500 bit × 0.0001220703125 = 0.0610351563 KiB

500 bits = 0.0610351563 kibs.

Frequently asked questions

How many kibs are in 1 bit?

1 bit equals 0.0001220703 kibs, using the exact defined conversion factor rather than an estimate.

How do I convert bit to kib?

Multiply the bit value by 0.0001220703125. The converter above does this instantly to whatever precision you set.

How do I convert kib back to bit?

Use the reverse factor: 1 kib equals 8,192 bits. You can also use the swap control in the converter above to flip the direction instantly.

What is a bit?

Bit (bit) is a unit of digital storage.

What is a kib?

KiB (KiB) is a unit of digital storage.

Is the bit to kib conversion exact?

Yes. Both bit and kib are defined by fixed standards rather than physical artifacts, so the factor of 0.0001220703125 used above is exact to as many digits as you choose to display.
