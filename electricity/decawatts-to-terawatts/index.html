About Converting Decawatts to Terawatts

Decawatt and Terawatt both belong to the electricity family — voltage, current, resistance, power, or energy — units that show up constantly when working across the different standards used by household wiring (120V/240V depending on region), automotive electrical systems (12V/24V), and electronics design. Specifically: Decawatt is 10 watt units, and terawatt is 1000000000000 watt units.

Formula

terawatts = decawatts × 1e-11

This factor comes from each unit's defined relationship to the category's base unit: 1 decawatt equals 10 base units, and 1 terawatt equals 1e+12 base units, so dividing one by the other gives the direct decawatt-to-terawatt factor of 1e-11.

Simple example

1 daW × 1e-11 = 1e-11 TW

1 decawatt = 1e-11 terawatts.

Real-world example

120 daW × 1e-11 = 1.2e-9 TW

120 decawatts = 1.2e-9 terawatts.

Frequently asked questions

How many terawatts are in 1 decawatt?

1 decawatt equals 1e-11 terawatts, using the exact defined conversion factor rather than an estimate.

How do I convert decawatt to terawatt?

Multiply the decawatt value by 1e-11. The converter above does this instantly to whatever precision you set.

How do I convert terawatt back to decawatt?

Use the reverse factor: 1 terawatt equals 100,000,000,000 decawatts. You can also use the swap control in the converter above to flip the direction instantly.

What is a decawatt?

What is a terawatt?

Terawatt (TW) is a unit of electricity.

Is the decawatt to terawatt conversion exact?

Yes. Both decawatt and terawatt are defined by fixed standards rather than physical artifacts, so the factor of 1e-11 used above is exact to as many digits as you choose to display.

What's the difference between voltage, current, and resistance?

Voltage (volts) is the electrical potential difference driving current through a circuit. Current (amperes) is the rate of flow of electric charge. Resistance (ohms) is how much a component opposes that flow. They're related by Ohm's law (V = I × R), but they are distinct physical quantities, and this page converts between units of just one of them.
