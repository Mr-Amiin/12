About Converting Femtoohms to Ohms

Femtoohm and Ohm both belong to the electricity family — voltage, current, resistance, power, or energy — units that show up constantly when working across the different standards used by household wiring (120V/240V depending on region), automotive electrical systems (12V/24V), and electronics design. Specifically: Femtoohm is 1e-15 ohm units, and ohm is the SI unit of electrical resistance.

Formula

ohms = femtoohms × 1e-15

This factor comes from each unit's defined relationship to the category's base unit: 1 femtoohm equals 1e-15 base units, and 1 ohm equals 1 base unit, so dividing one by the other gives the direct femtoohm-to-ohm factor of 1e-15.

Simple example

1 fOhm × 1e-15 = 1e-15 Ohm

1 femtoohm = 1e-15 ohms.

Real-world example

120 fOhm × 1e-15 = 1.2e-13 Ohm

120 femtoohms = 1.2e-13 ohms.

Understanding the Femtoohm (fOhm)

Understanding the Ohm

Ohm (Ohm) measures electrical resistance. The SI derived unit of electrical resistance, equal to one volt per ampere. It is classified as a SI derived unit.

Where it's used: Universal — SI derived unit used worldwide

Uses of the Ohm today

  • Resistor and circuit-component ratings
  • Electrical wiring and grounding specifications
  • Audio equipment impedance ratings

History of the Ohm

The ohm is named after Georg Simon Ohm (1789–1854), a German physicist who published Ohm's law in 1827, describing the relationship between voltage, current, and resistance (V = I × R) — the relationship the unit itself is defined through.

Historical origin. Ohm was not the invention of a single named person; it developed through the process described above.

Frequently asked questions

How many ohms are in 1 femtoohm?

1 femtoohm equals 1e-15 ohms, using the exact defined conversion factor rather than an estimate.

How do I convert femtoohm to ohm?

Multiply the femtoohm value by 1e-15. The converter above does this instantly to whatever precision you set.

How do I convert ohm back to femtoohm?

Use the reverse factor: 1 ohm equals 1e+15 femtoohms. You can also use the swap control in the converter above to flip the direction instantly.

What is a femtoohm?

Femtoohm (fOhm) is a unit of electricity.

What is a ohm?

Ohm (Ohm) measures electrical resistance. The SI derived unit of electrical resistance, equal to one volt per ampere. It is classified as a SI derived unit.

Is the femtoohm to ohm conversion exact?

Yes. Both femtoohm and ohm are defined by fixed standards rather than physical artifacts, so the factor of 1e-15 used above is exact to as many digits as you choose to display.

What's the difference between voltage, current, and resistance?

Voltage (volts) is the electrical potential difference driving current through a circuit. Current (amperes) is the rate of flow of electric charge. Resistance (ohms) is how much a component opposes that flow. They're related by Ohm's law (V = I × R), but they are distinct physical quantities, and this page converts between units of just one of them.
