About Converting Cubic Centimeters to Femtoliters

Cubic Centimeter and Femtoliter appear side by side at fuel pumps and on beverage labels, since the US measures liquids in gallons while most of the world uses liters. Specifically: Cubic metric volume unit, and femtoliter is 1e-15 liter units.

Formula

femtoliters = cubic centimeters × 1e+12

This factor comes from each unit's defined relationship to the category's base unit: 1 cubic centimeter equals 0.000001 base units, and 1 femtoliter equals 1e-18 base units, so dividing one by the other gives the direct cubic centimeter-to-femtoliter factor of 1e+12.

Simple example

1 cm3 × 1e+12 = 1e+12 fL

1 cubic centimeter = 1e+12 femtoliters.

Real-world example

10 cm3 × 1e+12 = 1e+13 fL

10 cubic centimeters = 1e+13 femtoliters.
