About Converting Centiliters to Metric teaspoons

Centiliter and Metric teaspoon appear side by side at fuel pumps and on beverage labels, since the US measures liquids in gallons while most of the world uses liters. Specifically: Centiliter is 0.01 liter units, and a metric teaspoon is 5 milliliters.

Formula

metric teaspoons = centiliters × 2

This factor comes from each unit's defined relationship to the category's base unit: 1 centiliter equals 0.00001 base units, and 1 metric teaspoon equals 0.000005 base units, so dividing one by the other gives the direct centiliter-to-metric teaspoon factor of 2.

Simple example

1 cL × 2 = 2 metric tsp

1 centiliter = 2 metric teaspoons.

Real-world example

10 cL × 2 = 20 metric tsp

10 centiliters = 20 metric teaspoons.
