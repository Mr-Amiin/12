About Converting Bushels to Pints

Bushel and Pint appear side by side at fuel pumps and on beverage labels, since the US measures liquids in gallons while most of the world uses liters. Specifically: A US bushel is about 35.239 liters, and a US liquid pint is one eighth of a US gallon.

Formula

pints = bushels × 74.4734199134

This factor comes from each unit's defined relationship to the category's base unit: 1 bushel equals 0.0352390701669 base units, and 1 pint equals 0.000473176473 base units, so dividing one by the other gives the direct bushel-to-pint factor of 74.4734199134.

Understanding the Bushel (bu)

A US bushel is about 35.239 liters.

Understanding the Pint

Pint (pt) measures volume. The US liquid pint is one-eighth of a US gallon: exactly 0.473176473 liters. It is classified as a US customary unit.

Where it's used: United States (beer serving, cooking, dairy)

Uses of the Pint today

  • US beer and beverage serving sizes
  • US recipe measurement
  • US dairy packaging (pint containers)

History of the Pint

The pint follows the same English wine-gallon lineage as the US gallon and quart, retained by the US after Britain's 1824 switch to a different imperial gallon standard.

Historical origin. Pint was not the invention of a single named person; it developed through the process described above.
