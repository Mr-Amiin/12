About Converting Nanoliters to Hectoliters

Nanoliter and Hectoliter appear side by side at fuel pumps and on beverage labels, since the US measures liquids in gallons while most of the world uses liters. Specifically: Nanoliter is 1e-9 liter units, and hectoliter is 100 liter units.

Formula

hectoliters = nanoliters × 1e-11

This factor comes from each unit's defined relationship to the category's base unit: 1 nanoliter equals 1e-12 base units, and 1 hectoliter equals 0.1 base units, so dividing one by the other gives the direct nanoliter-to-hectoliter factor of 1e-11.

Simple example

1 nL × 1e-11 = 1e-11 hL

1 nanoliter = 1e-11 hectoliters.
