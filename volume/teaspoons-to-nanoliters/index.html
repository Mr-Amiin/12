About Converting Teaspoons to Nanoliters

Teaspoon and Nanoliter appear side by side at fuel pumps and on beverage labels, since the US measures liquids in gallons while most of the world uses liters. Teaspoon is most often used for us recipe and cooking measurement for small quantities.

Formula

nanoliters = teaspoons × 4928921.59375

This factor comes from each unit's defined relationship to the category's base unit: 1 teaspoon equals 0.00000492892159375 base units, and 1 nanoliter equals 1e-12 base units, so dividing one by the other gives the direct teaspoon-to-nanoliter factor of 4928921.59375.

Understanding the Teaspoon

Teaspoon (tsp) measures volume. The US teaspoon is exactly 4.92892159375 milliliters — 1/3 of a US tablespoon and 1/48 of a US cup. It is classified as a US customary unit.
