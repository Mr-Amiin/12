About Converting Petaliters to Teraliters

Petaliter and Teraliter appear side by side at fuel pumps and on beverage labels, since the US measures liquids in gallons while most of the world uses liters. Specifically: Petaliter is 1000000000000000 liter units, and teraliter is 1000000000000 liter units.

Formula

teraliters = petaliters × 1000

This factor comes from each unit's defined relationship to the category's base unit: 1 petaliter equals 1e+12 base units, and 1 teraliter equals 1000000000 base units, so dividing one by the other gives the direct petaliter-to-teraliter factor of 1000.
