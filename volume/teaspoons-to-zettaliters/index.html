About Converting Teaspoons to Zettaliters

Teaspoon and Zettaliter appear side by side at fuel pumps and on beverage labels, since the US measures liquids in gallons while most of the world uses liters. Teaspoon is most often used for us recipe and cooking measurement for small quantities.

Understanding the Teaspoon

Teaspoon (tsp) measures volume. The US teaspoon is exactly 4.92892159375 milliliters — 1/3 of a US tablespoon and 1/48 of a US cup. It is classified as a US customary unit.
