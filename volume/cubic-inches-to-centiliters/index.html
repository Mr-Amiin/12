About Converting Cubic inches to Centiliters

Cubic inch and Centiliter appear side by side at fuel pumps and on beverage labels, since the US measures liquids in gallons while most of the world uses liters. Specifically: A cubic inch is the volume of a one-inch cube, and centiliter is 0.01 liter units.

centiliters = cubic inches × 1.6387064
