About Converting Fluid ounces to Hectoliters

Fluid ounce and Hectoliter appear side by side at fuel pumps and on beverage labels, since the US measures liquids in gallons while most of the world uses liters. Fluid ounce is most often used for us beverage container sizing (soda, water bottles).

hectoliters = fluid ounces × 0.000295735295625

Understanding the Fluid ounce

Fluid ounce (fl oz) measures volume. The US fluid ounce is 1/128 of a US gallon: exactly 29.5735295625 milliliters. It is classified as a US customary unit.

Where it's used: United States (beverage, nutrition labeling)

Uses of the Fluid ounce today

  • US beverage container sizing (soda, water bottles)
  • US nutrition and medication dosing labels

History of the Fluid ounce

The US fluid ounce follows from the same English wine-gallon system as the rest of the US liquid volume units, defined as a consistent fraction of the gallon.

Historical origin. Fluid ounce was not the invention of a single named person; it developed through the process described above.

Understanding the Hectoliter (hL)

Hectoliter is 100 liter units.
