About Converting Bushels to Tablespoons

Bushel and Tablespoon appear side by side at fuel pumps and on beverage labels, since the US measures liquids in gallons while most of the world uses liters. Specifically: A US bushel is about 35.239 liters, and a US tablespoon is half a US fluid ounce.

Formula

tablespoons = bushels × 2383.14943723

This factor comes from each unit's defined relationship to the category's base unit: 1 bushel equals 0.0352390701669 base units, and 1 tablespoon equals 0.0000147867647813 base units, so dividing one by the other gives the direct bushel-to-tablespoon factor of 2383.14943723.

Understanding the Tablespoon

Tablespoon (tbsp) measures volume. The US tablespoon is exactly 14.78676478125 milliliters — 3 US teaspoons, or 1/16 of a US cup. It is classified as a US customary unit.
