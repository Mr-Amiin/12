About Converting Cubic Kilometers to Cubic Zettameters

Cubic Kilometer and Cubic Zettameter appear side by side at fuel pumps and on beverage labels, since the US measures liquids in gallons while most of the world uses liters. Both are cubic metric volume unit units.

Formula

cubic zettameters = cubic kilometers × 1e-54

This factor comes from each unit's defined relationship to the category's base unit: 1 cubic kilometer equals 1000000000 base units, and 1 cubic zettameter equals 1e+63 base units, so dividing one by the other gives the direct cubic kilometer-to-cubic zettameter factor of 1e-54.

Simple example

1 km3 × 1e-54 = 1e-54 Zm3

1 cubic kilometer = 1e-54 cubic zettameters.

Real-world example
