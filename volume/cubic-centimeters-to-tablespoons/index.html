About Converting Cubic Centimeters to Tablespoons

Cubic Centimeter and Tablespoon appear side by side at fuel pumps and on beverage labels, since the US measures liquids in gallons while most of the world uses liters. Specifically: Cubic metric volume unit, and a US tablespoon is half a US fluid ounce.

Formula

tablespoons = cubic centimeters × 0.0676280454037

Understanding the Tablespoon

Tablespoon (tbsp) measures volume. The US tablespoon is exactly 14.78676478125 milliliters — 3 US teaspoons, or 1/16 of a US cup. It is classified as a US customary unit.
