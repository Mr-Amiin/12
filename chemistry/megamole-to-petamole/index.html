About Converting Megamoles to Petamoles

Megamole and Petamole both measure mass concentration — how much of a substance is dissolved or dispersed in a given volume — a core calculation for preparing lab solutions, checking water quality against a regulatory limit, or formulating a pharmaceutical dose. Specifically: Megamole is 1000000 mole units, and petamole is 1000000000000000 mole units.

Formula

petamoles = megamoles × 1e-9

This factor comes from each unit's defined relationship to the category's base unit: 1 megamole equals 1000000 base units, and 1 petamole equals 1e+15 base units, so dividing one by the other gives the direct megamole-to-petamole factor of 1e-9.

What's the difference between mass concentration and molarity?

Mass concentration expresses how much mass of a substance is present per unit volume (for example, grams per liter). Molarity expresses the amount in moles per liter instead, which accounts for the substance's molecular weight. This category converts mass-concentration units.
