About Converting Grams per Cubic Meter to Parts per billion water

Gram per Cubic Meter and Parts per billion water both measure mass concentration — how much of a substance is dissolved or dispersed in a given volume — a core calculation for preparing lab solutions, checking water quality against a regulatory limit, or formulating a pharmaceutical dose. Specifically: Mass concentration, and approximate ug/L in water.

Formula

parts per billion water = grams per cubic meter × 1000

This factor comes from each unit's defined relationship to the category's base unit: 1 gram per cubic meter equals 0.001 base units, and 1 parts per billion water equals 0.000001 base units, so dividing one by the other gives the direct gram per cubic meter-to-parts per billion water factor of 1000.

What's the difference between mass concentration and molarity?

Mass concentration expresses how much mass of a substance is present per unit volume (for example, grams per liter). Molarity expresses the amount in moles per liter instead, which accounts for the substance's molecular weight. This category converts mass-concentration units.
