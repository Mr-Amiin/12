About Converting Ounces per Gallon to Pounds per Milliliter

Ounce per Gallon and Pound per Milliliter both measure mass concentration — how much of a substance is dissolved or dispersed in a given volume — a core calculation for preparing lab solutions, checking water quality against a regulatory limit, or formulating a pharmaceutical dose. Ounce per Gallon is most often used for used to express dilution rates on many us pesticide, herbicide, and household cleaning product labels, which commonly specify a number of fluid ounces of concentrate per gallon of water.

Understanding the Ounce per Gallon

Ounce per Gallon (oz/gal) measures concentration (dilution rate). A US customary way to express how much concentrate is mixed into each gallon of a diluted solution. It is classified as a US customary concentration unit.

Where it's used: United States

What's the difference between mass concentration and molarity?
