About Converting Parts per billion water to Pounds per Liter

Parts per billion water and Pound per Liter both measure mass concentration — how much of a substance is dissolved or dispersed in a given volume — a core calculation for preparing lab solutions, checking water quality against a regulatory limit, or formulating a pharmaceutical dose. Formula

pounds per liter = parts per billion water × 2.204623e-9

What's the difference between mass concentration and molarity?

Mass concentration expresses how much mass of a substance is present per unit volume (for example, grams per liter). Molarity expresses the amount in moles per liter instead, which accounts for the substance's molecular weight. This category converts mass-concentration units.
