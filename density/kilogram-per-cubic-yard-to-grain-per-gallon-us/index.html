About Converting Kilograms per Cubic yard to Grains per Gallon

Kilogram per Cubic yard and Grain per Gallon both measure density — mass per unit volume — a property used to identify materials, check manufacturing quality, and predict whether something floats or sinks. As a fixed reference point, water has a density of exactly 1000 kg/m³ (1 g/cm³, 1 g/mL) at its temperature of maximum density, which is why many density figures in science and engineering are quoted relative to water. Kilogram per Cubic yard is most often used for can be used to express the density of bulk construction materials such as gravel, soil, or aggregate — in practice, us construction and landscaping references more commonly express this as tons per cubic yard or pounds per cubic foot.

What's the difference between density and mass concentration?

Density is the mass of a pure substance or material per unit volume. Mass concentration is the mass of one component — like a dissolved solute — within a mixture's total volume. The two use the same kind of units but describe different physical situations.
