About Converting Decapascals to Micropascals

Decapascal and Micropascal both show up on pressure gauges — psi is standard on US tire and tool specifications, while bar is the everyday pressure unit across most of the rest of the world. Specifically: Decapascal is 10 pascal units, and micropascal is 0.000001 pascal units.

Formula

micropascals = decapascals × 10000000

This factor comes from each unit's defined relationship to the category's base unit: 1 decapascal equals 10 base units, and 1 micropascal equals 0.000001 base units, so dividing one by the other gives the direct decapascal-to-micropascal factor of 10000000.

Simple example

1 daPa × 10000000 = 10,000,000 uPa

1 decapascal = 10,000,000 micropascals.
