About Converting KSI to Attopascals

KSI and Attopascal both show up on pressure gauges — psi is standard on US tire and tool specifications, while bar is the everyday pressure unit across most of the rest of the world. Specifically: Kips per square inch, and attopascal is 1e-18 pascal units.

Formula

attopascals = ksi × 6.894757e+24

This factor comes from each unit's defined relationship to the category's base unit: 1 ksi equals 6894757.29317 base units, and 1 attopascal equals 1e-18 base units, so dividing one by the other gives the direct ksi-to-attopascal factor of 6.894757e+24.

Simple example

1 ksi × 6.894757e+24 = 6.894757e+24 aPa
