About Converting Torrs to Ronnapascals

Torr and Ronnapascal both show up on pressure gauges — psi is standard on US tire and tool specifications, while bar is the everyday pressure unit across most of the rest of the world. Specifically: A torr is 1/760 standard atmosphere, and ronnapascal is 1e+27 pascal units.

Formula

ronnapascals = torrs × 1.333224e-25
