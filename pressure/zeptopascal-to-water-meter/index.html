About Converting Zeptopascals to Meter of waters

Zeptopascal and Meter of water both show up on pressure gauges — psi is standard on US tire and tool specifications, while bar is the everyday pressure unit across most of the rest of the world. Specifically: Zeptopascal is 1e-21 pascal units, and meter of water column.

Formula

meter of waters = zeptopascals × 1.019716e-25

This factor comes from each unit's defined relationship to the category's base unit: 1 zeptopascal equals 1e-21 base units, and 1 meter of water equals 9806.65 base units, so dividing one by the other gives the direct zeptopascal-to-meter of water factor of 1.019716e-25.
