About Converting Yottapascals to KSI

Yottapascal and KSI both show up on pressure gauges — psi is standard on US tire and tool specifications, while bar is the everyday pressure unit across most of the rest of the world. Specifically: Yottapascal is 1e+24 pascal units, and kips per square inch.

Formula

ksi = yottapascals × 1.450377e+17

This factor comes from each unit's defined relationship to the category's base unit: 1 yottapascal equals 1e+24 base units, and 1 ksi equals 6894757.29317 base units, so dividing one by the other gives the direct yottapascal-to-ksi factor of 1.450377e+17.
