About Converting Zettapascals to Technical atmospheres

Zettapascal and Technical atmosphere both show up on pressure gauges — psi is standard on US tire and tool specifications, while bar is the everyday pressure unit across most of the rest of the world. Specifically: Zettapascal is 1e+21 pascal units, and technical atmosphere is kilogram-force per square centimeter.

technical atmospheres = zettapascals × 1.019716e+16
