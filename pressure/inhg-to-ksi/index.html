About Converting inHgs to KSI

inHg and KSI both show up on pressure gauges — psi is standard on US tire and tool specifications, while bar is the everyday pressure unit across most of the rest of the world. Specifically: Inches of mercury, and kips per square inch.

Formula

ksi = inhgs × 0.000491154199634

This factor comes from each unit's defined relationship to the category's base unit: 1 inhg equals 3386.389 base units, and 1 ksi equals 6894757.29317 base units, so dividing one by the other gives the direct inhg-to-ksi factor of 0.000491154199634.
