About this conversion

This area converter turns acre into hectare using the exact defined relationship between the two units, not an approximation. A farmer with 25 acres owns approximately 10.12 hectares. The acre originates from medieval England as the area a yoke of oxen could plow in a day, later standardized at 43,560 square feet. The hectare was introduced with the metric system as 10,000 square meters, or the area of a square 100 meters on each side. Because both units are built on internationally standardized constants, the same acre-to-hectare factor works whether you're doing a quick everyday estimate or a calculation that needs to be precise.
