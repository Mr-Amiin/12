Frequently asked questions

How do you convert liter to us gallon?

Use the formula gal = L × 0.264172052358. One liter equals 0.264172052358 us gallons.

What is Liter?

The liter is a metric unit of volume used for liquids and gases.

What is US gallon?

The US gallon is a customary unit of liquid volume.

Is the liter to us gallon conversion exact?

Yes. Liter and US gallon are both defined against internationally agreed constants, so the conversion factor used here is exact -- any rounding you see comes only from the number of decimal places displayed, not from the relationship itself.

How do I convert us gallon back to liter?

Use the reverse formula L = gal ÷ 0.264172052358. This site's reverse converter does the same calculation automatically.

About this conversion

This volume converter turns liter into us gallon using the exact defined relationship between the two units, not an approximation. A 10-liter fuel tank holds about 2.64 US gallons. The liter was introduced with the metric system in 1795 and is defined as exactly one cubic decimeter. The US gallon derives from an old English wine gallon measure, distinct from the larger imperial gallon used in the UK. Because both units are built on internationally standardized constants, the same liter-to-us gallon factor works whether you're doing a quick everyday estimate or a calculation that needs to be precise.
