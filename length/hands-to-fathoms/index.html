About Converting Hands to Fathoms

Hand and Fathom both measure length. Specifically: A hand is four inches, often used for horse height, and a fathom is six feet.

Formula

fathoms = hands × 0.0555555555556

This factor comes from each unit's defined relationship to the category's base unit: 1 hand equals 0.1016 base units, and 1 fathom equals 1.8288 base units, so dividing one by the other gives the direct hand-to-fathom factor of 0.0555555555556.

Simple example

1 hh × 0.05555555556 = 0.0555555556 ftm

1 hand = 0.0555555556 fathoms.

Real-world example

10 hh × 0.05555555556 = 0.5555555556 ftm
