About Converting Gigameters to Rods

Gigameter and Rod both measure length. Specifically: Gigameter is 1000000000 meter units, and a rod is 16.5 feet.

Formula

rods = gigameters × 198838781.516

This factor comes from each unit's defined relationship to the category's base unit: 1 gigameter equals 1000000000 base units, and 1 rod equals 5.0292 base units, so dividing one by the other gives the direct gigameter-to-rod factor of 198838781.516.

1 gigameter = 198,838,781.5 rods.
