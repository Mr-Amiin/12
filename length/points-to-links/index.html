About Converting Points to Links

Point and Link both measure length. Specifically: A desktop publishing point is 1/72 inch, and a surveyor's link is 1/100 of a chain.

Formula

links = points × 0.00175364758709

This factor comes from each unit's defined relationship to the category's base unit: 1 point equals 0.0003527777778 base units, and 1 link equals 0.201168 base units, so dividing one by the other gives the direct point-to-link factor of 0.00175364758709.

Simple example

1 pt × 0.001753647587 = 0.0017536476 li

1 point = 0.0017536476 links.

Real-world example

10 pt × 0.001753647587 = 0.0175364759 li
