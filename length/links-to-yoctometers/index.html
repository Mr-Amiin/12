About Converting Links to Yoctometers

Link and Yoctometer both measure length. Specifically: A surveyor's link is 1/100 of a chain, and yoctometer is 1e-24 meter units.

Formula

yoctometers = links × 2.01168e+23

This factor comes from each unit's defined relationship to the category's base unit: 1 link equals 0.201168 base units, and 1 yoctometer equals 1e-24 base units, so dividing one by the other gives the direct link-to-yoctometer factor of 2.01168e+23.

Simple example

1 li × 2.01168e+23 = 2.01168e+23 ym

1 link = 2.01168e+23 yoctometers.

Real-world example
