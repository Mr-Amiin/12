About Converting Terameters to Gigameters

Terameter and Gigameter both measure length. Specifically: Terameter is 1000000000000 meter units, and gigameter is 1000000000 meter units.

Formula

gigameters = terameters × 1000

This factor comes from each unit's defined relationship to the category's base unit: 1 terameter equals 1e+12 base units, and 1 gigameter equals 1000000000 base units, so dividing one by the other gives the direct terameter-to-gigameter factor of 1000.

1 terameter = 1,000 gigameters.
