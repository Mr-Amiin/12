About Converting Points to Ronnameters

Point and Ronnameter both measure length. Specifically: A desktop publishing point is 1/72 inch, and ronnameter is 1e+27 meter units.

Formula

ronnameters = points × 3.527778e-31

This factor comes from each unit's defined relationship to the category's base unit: 1 point equals 0.0003527777778 base units, and 1 ronnameter equals 1e+27 base units, so dividing one by the other gives the direct point-to-ronnameter factor of 3.527778e-31.

Simple example

1 pt × 3.527778e-31 = 3.527778e-31 Rm

1 point = 3.527778e-31 ronnameters.

Real-world example
