About Converting Femtometers to Zettameters

Femtometer and Zettameter both measure length. Specifically: Femtometer is 1e-15 meter units, and zettameter is 1e+21 meter units.

This factor comes from each unit's defined relationship to the category's base unit: 1 femtometer equals 1e-15 base units, and 1 zettameter equals 1e+21 base units, so dividing one by the other gives the direct femtometer-to-zettameter factor of 1e-36.
