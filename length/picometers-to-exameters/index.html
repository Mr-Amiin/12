About Converting Picometers to Exameters

This factor comes from each unit's defined relationship to the category's base unit: 1 picometer equals 1e-12 base units, and 1 exameter equals 1e+18 base units, so dividing one by the other gives the direct picometer-to-exameter factor of 1e-30.
