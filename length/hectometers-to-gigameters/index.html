About Converting Hectometers to Gigameters

Hectometer and Gigameter both measure length. Specifically: Hectometer is 100 meter units, and gigameter is 1000000000 meter units.

Formula

gigameters = hectometers × 1e-7

This factor comes from each unit's defined relationship to the category's base unit: 1 hectometer equals 100 base units, and 1 gigameter equals 1000000000 base units, so dividing one by the other gives the direct hectometer-to-gigameter factor of 1e-7.

Simple example

1 hm × 1e-7 = 1e-7 Gm

1 hectometer = 1e-7 gigameters.

Real-world example

10 hm × 1e-7 = 0.000001 Gm

10 hectometers = 0.000001 gigameters.
