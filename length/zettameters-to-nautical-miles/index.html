Understanding the Nautical mile

Nautical mile (nmi) measures distance. The international nautical mile is defined as exactly 1,852 meters, standardized in 1929. It is classified as a Non-SI unit accepted for use with SI (maritime/aviation).

Where it's used: Used internationally in maritime navigation and aviation.

Uses of the Nautical mile today

  • Marine and aviation distance and navigation
  • Basis for the knot (nautical miles per hour)

History of the Nautical mile

The nautical mile was historically defined as one minute of latitude along a meridian, directly tying sea navigation to the Earth's geometry, which made it practical for chart-based navigation using a ship's known latitude. The International Hydrographic Bureau standardized it at exactly 1,852 meters in 1929, a value close to the traditional latitude-based definitions used by different countries.

Historical origin. Nautical mile was not the invention of a single named person; it developed through the process described above.
