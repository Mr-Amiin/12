Understanding the Millimeter

Millimeter (mm) measures distance. A millimeter is one-thousandth of a meter. It is classified as a SI derived (metric prefix) unit.

Where it's used: Standard small/precision length unit worldwide, including in US engineering and manufacturing

Uses of the Millimeter today

  • Engineering drawings and manufacturing tolerances worldwide (including in the US)
  • Rainfall measurement in most countries
  • Small-scale everyday measurement (screen sizes, hardware)

History of the Millimeter

The millimeter follows from the meter via the 'milli-' SI prefix (10⁻³). It is notable as one of the few metric units in routine US technical/engineering use, since precision manufacturing and electronics are often specified in millimeters even in the US.

Historical origin. Millimeter was not the invention of a single named person; it developed through the process described above.
