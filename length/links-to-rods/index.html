About Converting Links to Rods

Link and Rod both measure length. Specifically: A surveyor's link is 1/100 of a chain, and a rod is 16.5 feet.

Formula

rods = links × 0.04

This factor comes from each unit's defined relationship to the category's base unit: 1 link equals 0.201168 base units, and 1 rod equals 5.0292 base units, so dividing one by the other gives the direct link-to-rod factor of 0.04.

Simple example

1 li × 0.04 = 0.04 rd

1 link = 0.04 rods.

Real-world example

10 li × 0.04 = 0.4 rd

10 links = 0.4 rods.

Frequently asked questions

How many rods are in 1 link?

1 link equals 0.04 rods, using the exact defined conversion factor rather than an estimate.

How do I convert link to rod?

Multiply the link value by 0.04. The converter above does this instantly to whatever precision you set.

How do I convert rod back to link?

Use the reverse factor: 1 rod equals 25 links. You can also use the swap control in the converter above to flip the direction instantly.

What is a link?

What is a rod?

Is the link to rod conversion exact?

Yes. Both link and rod are defined by fixed standards rather than physical artifacts, so the factor of 0.04 used above is exact to as many digits as you choose to display.
