About Converting Yards to Links

Yard and Link both measure length. Yard is most often used for american football field measurement.

Formula

links = yards × 4.54545454545

This factor comes from each unit's defined relationship to the category's base unit: 1 yard equals 0.9144 base units, and 1 link equals 0.201168 base units, so dividing one by the other gives the direct yard-to-link factor of 4.54545454545.

Real-world example

Understanding the Yard

Yard (yd) measures distance. The international yard is defined as exactly 0.9144 meters. It is classified as a US/imperial customary unit.

Where it's used: United States and United Kingdom (sports fields, fabric, some construction)

Uses of the Yard today

  • American football field measurement
  • Fabric and textile measurement
  • Golf and other sports distances in the US/UK

History of the Yard

The yard has English origins possibly tied to a standard 'gird' or arm's-length measure; it was fixed at exactly 0.9144 meters by the 1959 International Yard and Pound Agreement, the same treaty that standardized the foot, inch, mile, and pound.

Historical origin. Yard was not the invention of a single named person; it developed through the process described above.
