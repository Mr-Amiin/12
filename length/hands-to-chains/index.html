About Converting Hands to Chains

Hand and Chain both measure length. Specifically: A hand is four inches, often used for horse height, and a surveyor's chain is 66 feet.

Formula

chains = hands × 0.00505050505051

This factor comes from each unit's defined relationship to the category's base unit: 1 hand equals 0.1016 base units, and 1 chain equals 20.1168 base units, so dividing one by the other gives the direct hand-to-chain factor of 0.00505050505051.

Simple example

1 hh × 0.005050505051 = 0.0050505051 ch

1 hand = 0.0050505051 chains.

Real-world example

10 hh × 0.005050505051 = 0.0505050505 ch

10 hands = 0.0505050505 chains.
