About Converting Picowatts to Yottawatts

Picowatt and Yottawatt both describe power output, and the conversion is a routine part of comparing an engine or motor's spec sheet across countries that use different power units. Specifically: Picowatt is 1e-12 watt units, and yottawatt is 1e+24 watt units.

Formula

yottawatts = picowatts × 1e-36

This factor comes from each unit's defined relationship to the category's base unit: 1 picowatt equals 1e-12 base units, and 1 yottawatt equals 1e+24 base units, so dividing one by the other gives the direct picowatt-to-yottawatt factor of 1e-36.

Simple example

1 pW × 1e-36 = 1e-36 YW

1 picowatt = 1e-36 yottawatts.

Real-world example

100 pW × 1e-36 = 1e-34 YW

100 picowatts = 1e-34 yottawatts.
