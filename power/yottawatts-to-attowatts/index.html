About Converting Yottawatts to Attowatts

Yottawatt and Attowatt both describe power output, and the conversion is a routine part of comparing an engine or motor's spec sheet across countries that use different power units. Specifically: Yottawatt is 1e+24 watt units, and attowatt is 1e-18 watt units.

Formula

attowatts = yottawatts × 1e+42

This factor comes from each unit's defined relationship to the category's base unit: 1 yottawatt equals 1e+24 base units, and 1 attowatt equals 1e-18 base units, so dividing one by the other gives the direct yottawatt-to-attowatt factor of 1e+42.

Simple example

1 YW × 1e+42 = 1e+42 aW

1 yottawatt = 1e+42 attowatts.

Real-world example

100 YW × 1e+42 = 1e+44 aW

100 yottawatts = 1e+44 attowatts.
