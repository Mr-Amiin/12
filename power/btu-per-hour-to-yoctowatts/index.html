About Converting BTU per hour to Yoctowatts

BTU per hour and Yoctowatt both describe power output, and the conversion is a routine part of comparing an engine or motor's spec sheet across countries that use different power units. Specifically: Power rate using BTU per hour, and yoctowatt is 1e-24 watt units.

Formula

yoctowatts = btu per hour × 2.930711e+23

This factor comes from each unit's defined relationship to the category's base unit: 1 btu per hour equals 0.293071070172 base units, and 1 yoctowatt equals 1e-24 base units, so dividing one by the other gives the direct btu per hour-to-yoctowatt factor of 2.930711e+23.
