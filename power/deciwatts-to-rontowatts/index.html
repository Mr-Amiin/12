About Converting Deciwatts to Rontowatts

Deciwatt and Rontowatt both describe power output, and the conversion is a routine part of comparing an engine or motor's spec sheet across countries that use different power units. Specifically: Deciwatt is 0.1 watt units, and rontowatt is 1e-27 watt units.

Formula

rontowatts = deciwatts × 1e+26

This factor comes from each unit's defined relationship to the category's base unit: 1 deciwatt equals 0.1 base units, and 1 rontowatt equals 1e-27 base units, so dividing one by the other gives the direct deciwatt-to-rontowatt factor of 1e+26.

Simple example

1 dW × 1e+26 = 1e+26 rW

1 deciwatt = 1e+26 rontowatts.

Real-world example

100 dW × 1e+26 = 1e+28 rW

100 deciwatts = 1e+28 rontowatts.
