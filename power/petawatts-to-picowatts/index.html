About Converting Petawatts to Picowatts

Petawatt and Picowatt both describe power output, and the conversion is a routine part of comparing an engine or motor's spec sheet across countries that use different power units. Specifically: Petawatt is 1000000000000000 watt units, and picowatt is 1e-12 watt units.

Formula

picowatts = petawatts × 1e+27

This factor comes from each unit's defined relationship to the category's base unit: 1 petawatt equals 1e+15 base units, and 1 picowatt equals 1e-12 base units, so dividing one by the other gives the direct petawatt-to-picowatt factor of 1e+27.

Simple example

1 PW × 1e+27 = 1e+27 pW

1 petawatt = 1e+27 picowatts.

Real-world example

100 PW × 1e+27 = 1e+29 pW

100 petawatts = 1e+29 picowatts.
