About Converting Exawatts to Decawatts

Exawatt and Decawatt both describe power output, and the conversion is a routine part of comparing an engine or motor's spec sheet across countries that use different power units. Specifically: Exawatt is 1000000000000000000 watt units, and decawatt is 10 watt units.

Formula

decawatts = exawatts × 1e+17

This factor comes from each unit's defined relationship to the category's base unit: 1 exawatt equals 1e+18 base units, and 1 decawatt equals 10 base units, so dividing one by the other gives the direct exawatt-to-decawatt factor of 1e+17.

Simple example

1 EW × 1e+17 = 1e+17 daW

1 exawatt = 1e+17 decawatts.

Real-world example

100 EW × 1e+17 = 1e+19 daW

100 exawatts = 1e+19 decawatts.
