About Converting Megawatts to Yottawatts

Megawatt and Yottawatt both describe power output, and the conversion is a routine part of comparing an engine or motor's spec sheet across countries that use different power units. Specifically: Megawatt is 1000000 watt units, and yottawatt is 1e+24 watt units.

Formula

yottawatts = megawatts × 1e-18

This factor comes from each unit's defined relationship to the category's base unit: 1 megawatt equals 1000000 base units, and 1 yottawatt equals 1e+24 base units, so dividing one by the other gives the direct megawatt-to-yottawatt factor of 1e-18.

Simple example

1 MW × 1e-18 = 1e-18 YW

1 megawatt = 1e-18 yottawatts.

Real-world example

100 MW × 1e-18 = 1e-16 YW

100 megawatts = 1e-16 yottawatts.
