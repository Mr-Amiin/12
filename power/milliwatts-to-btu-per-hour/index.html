About Converting Milliwatts to BTU per hour

Milliwatt and BTU per hour both describe power output, and the conversion is a routine part of comparing an engine or motor's spec sheet across countries that use different power units. Specifically: Milliwatt is 0.001 watt units, and power rate using BTU per hour.

Formula

btu per hour = milliwatts × 0.00341214163313

This factor comes from each unit's defined relationship to the category's base unit: 1 milliwatt equals 0.001 base units, and 1 btu per hour equals 0.293071070172 base units, so dividing one by the other gives the direct milliwatt-to-btu per hour factor of 0.00341214163313.
