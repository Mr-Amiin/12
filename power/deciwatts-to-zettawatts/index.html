About Converting Deciwatts to Zettawatts

Deciwatt and Zettawatt both describe power output, and the conversion is a routine part of comparing an engine or motor's spec sheet across countries that use different power units. Specifically: Deciwatt is 0.1 watt units, and zettawatt is 1e+21 watt units.

Formula

zettawatts = deciwatts × 1e-22

This factor comes from each unit's defined relationship to the category's base unit: 1 deciwatt equals 0.1 base units, and 1 zettawatt equals 1e+21 base units, so dividing one by the other gives the direct deciwatt-to-zettawatt factor of 1e-22.

Simple example

1 dW × 1e-22 = 1e-22 ZW

1 deciwatt = 1e-22 zettawatts.

Real-world example

100 dW × 1e-22 = 1e-20 ZW

100 deciwatts = 1e-20 zettawatts.
