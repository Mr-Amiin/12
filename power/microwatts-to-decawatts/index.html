About Converting Microwatts to Decawatts

Microwatt and Decawatt both describe power output, and the conversion is a routine part of comparing an engine or motor's spec sheet across countries that use different power units. Specifically: Microwatt is 0.000001 watt units, and decawatt is 10 watt units.

Formula

decawatts = microwatts × 1e-7

This factor comes from each unit's defined relationship to the category's base unit: 1 microwatt equals 0.000001 base units, and 1 decawatt equals 10 base units, so dividing one by the other gives the direct microwatt-to-decawatt factor of 1e-7.

Simple example

1 uW × 1e-7 = 1e-7 daW

1 microwatt = 1e-7 decawatts.

Real-world example

100 uW × 1e-7 = 0.00001 daW

100 microwatts = 0.00001 decawatts.
