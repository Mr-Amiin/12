About Converting Decawatts to Hectowatts

Decawatt and Hectowatt both describe power output, and the conversion is a routine part of comparing an engine or motor's spec sheet across countries that use different power units. Specifically: Decawatt is 10 watt units, and hectowatt is 100 watt units.

Formula

hectowatts = decawatts × 0.1

This factor comes from each unit's defined relationship to the category's base unit: 1 decawatt equals 10 base units, and 1 hectowatt equals 100 base units, so dividing one by the other gives the direct decawatt-to-hectowatt factor of 0.1.

Simple example

1 daW × 0.1 = 0.1 hW

1 decawatt = 0.1 hectowatts.

Real-world example

100 daW × 0.1 = 10 hW

100 decawatts = 10 hectowatts.
