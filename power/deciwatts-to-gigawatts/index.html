About Converting Deciwatts to Gigawatts

Deciwatt and Gigawatt both describe power output, and the conversion is a routine part of comparing an engine or motor's spec sheet across countries that use different power units. Specifically: Deciwatt is 0.1 watt units, and gigawatt is 1000000000 watt units.

Formula

gigawatts = deciwatts × 1e-10

This factor comes from each unit's defined relationship to the category's base unit: 1 deciwatt equals 0.1 base units, and 1 gigawatt equals 1000000000 base units, so dividing one by the other gives the direct deciwatt-to-gigawatt factor of 1e-10.

Simple example

1 dW × 1e-10 = 1e-10 GW

1 deciwatt = 1e-10 gigawatts.

Real-world example

100 dW × 1e-10 = 1e-8 GW

100 deciwatts = 1e-8 gigawatts.
