About Converting Nanowatts to Gigawatts

Nanowatt and Gigawatt both describe power output, and the conversion is a routine part of comparing an engine or motor's spec sheet across countries that use different power units. Specifically: Nanowatt is 1e-9 watt units, and gigawatt is 1000000000 watt units.

Formula

gigawatts = nanowatts × 1e-18

This factor comes from each unit's defined relationship to the category's base unit: 1 nanowatt equals 1e-9 base units, and 1 gigawatt equals 1000000000 base units, so dividing one by the other gives the direct nanowatt-to-gigawatt factor of 1e-18.

Simple example

1 nW × 1e-18 = 1e-18 GW

1 nanowatt = 1e-18 gigawatts.

Real-world example

100 nW × 1e-18 = 1e-16 GW

100 nanowatts = 1e-16 gigawatts.
