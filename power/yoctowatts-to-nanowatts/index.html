About Converting Yoctowatts to Nanowatts

Yoctowatt and Nanowatt both describe power output, and the conversion is a routine part of comparing an engine or motor's spec sheet across countries that use different power units. Specifically: Yoctowatt is 1e-24 watt units, and nanowatt is 1e-9 watt units.

Formula

nanowatts = yoctowatts × 1e-15

This factor comes from each unit's defined relationship to the category's base unit: 1 yoctowatt equals 1e-24 base units, and 1 nanowatt equals 1e-9 base units, so dividing one by the other gives the direct yoctowatt-to-nanowatt factor of 1e-15.

Simple example

1 yW × 1e-15 = 1e-15 nW

1 yoctowatt = 1e-15 nanowatts.

Real-world example

100 yW × 1e-15 = 1e-13 nW

100 yoctowatts = 1e-13 nanowatts.
