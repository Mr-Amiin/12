About Converting Yottawatts to Femtowatts

Yottawatt and Femtowatt both describe power output, and the conversion is a routine part of comparing an engine or motor's spec sheet across countries that use different power units. Specifically: Yottawatt is 1e+24 watt units, and femtowatt is 1e-15 watt units.

Formula

femtowatts = yottawatts × 1e+39

This factor comes from each unit's defined relationship to the category's base unit: 1 yottawatt equals 1e+24 base units, and 1 femtowatt equals 1e-15 base units, so dividing one by the other gives the direct yottawatt-to-femtowatt factor of 1e+39.

Simple example

1 YW × 1e+39 = 1e+39 fW

1 yottawatt = 1e+39 femtowatts.

Real-world example

100 YW × 1e+39 = 1e+41 fW

100 yottawatts = 1e+41 femtowatts.
