About Converting Dynes to Decinewtons

Dyne and Decinewton both measure force, and the conversion matters whenever an SI-trained engineer needs to compare notes with a US-trained one, or vice versa. Specifically: A dyne is a CGS force unit, and decinewton is 0.1 newton units.

Formula

decinewtons = dynes × 0.0001

This factor comes from each unit's defined relationship to the category's base unit: 1 dyne equals 0.00001 base units, and 1 decinewton equals 0.1 base units, so dividing one by the other gives the direct dyne-to-decinewton factor of 0.0001.

Simple example

1 dyn × 0.0001 = 0.0001 dN

1 dyne = 0.0001 decinewtons.

Real-world example

10 dyn × 0.0001 = 0.001 dN

10 dynes = 0.001 decinewtons.
