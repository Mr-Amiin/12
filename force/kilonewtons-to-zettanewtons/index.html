About Converting Kilonewtons to Zettanewtons

Kilonewton and Zettanewton both measure force, and the conversion matters whenever an SI-trained engineer needs to compare notes with a US-trained one, or vice versa. Specifically: Kilonewton is 1000 newton units, and zettanewton is 1e+21 newton units.

Formula

zettanewtons = kilonewtons × 1e-18

This factor comes from each unit's defined relationship to the category's base unit: 1 kilonewton equals 1000 base units, and 1 zettanewton equals 1e+21 base units, so dividing one by the other gives the direct kilonewton-to-zettanewton factor of 1e-18.
