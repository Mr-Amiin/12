Understanding the Newton

Newton (N) measures force. The newton is the SI derived unit of force: the force needed to accelerate a 1 kg mass at 1 meter per second squared. It is classified as a SI derived unit.

Where it's used: Standard scientific and engineering force unit worldwide

Uses of the Newton today

  • Physics and engineering force calculations
  • Material strength and structural engineering specifications
  • Product specifications (e.g. spring force, adhesive strength)

History of the Newton

The newton is named after Isaac Newton in recognition of his laws of motion, which define the relationship between force, mass, and acceleration that the unit embodies. It was formally adopted as the SI derived unit of force as part of the broader 20th-century SI system.

Historical origin. Newton was not the invention of a single named person; it developed through the process described above.
