About Converting Piconewtons to Femtonewtons

Piconewton and Femtonewton both measure force, and the conversion matters whenever an SI-trained engineer needs to compare notes with a US-trained one, or vice versa. Specifically: Piconewton is 1e-12 newton units, and femtonewton is 1e-15 newton units.

Formula

femtonewtons = piconewtons × 1000

This factor comes from each unit's defined relationship to the category's base unit: 1 piconewton equals 1e-12 base units, and 1 femtonewton equals 1e-15 base units, so dividing one by the other gives the direct piconewton-to-femtonewton factor of 1000.

Simple example

1 pN × 1000 = 1,000 fN

1 piconewton = 1,000 femtonewtons.
