About Converting Piconewtons to Micronewtons

Piconewton and Micronewton both measure force, and the conversion matters whenever an SI-trained engineer needs to compare notes with a US-trained one, or vice versa. Specifically: Piconewton is 1e-12 newton units, and micronewton is 0.000001 newton units.

Formula

micronewtons = piconewtons × 0.000001

This factor comes from each unit's defined relationship to the category's base unit: 1 piconewton equals 1e-12 base units, and 1 micronewton equals 0.000001 base units, so dividing one by the other gives the direct piconewton-to-micronewton factor of 0.000001.
