About Converting Giganewtons to Kilonewtons

Giganewton and Kilonewton both measure force, and the conversion matters whenever an SI-trained engineer needs to compare notes with a US-trained one, or vice versa. Specifically: Giganewton is 1000000000 newton units, and kilonewton is 1000 newton units.

Formula

kilonewtons = giganewtons × 1000000

This factor comes from each unit's defined relationship to the category's base unit: 1 giganewton equals 1000000000 base units, and 1 kilonewton equals 1000 base units, so dividing one by the other gives the direct giganewton-to-kilonewton factor of 1000000.

Simple example

1 GN × 1000000 = 1,000,000 kN

1 giganewton = 1,000,000 kilonewtons.

Real-world example

10 GN × 1000000 = 10,000,000 kN

10 giganewtons = 10,000,000 kilonewtons.
