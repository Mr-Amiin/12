About Converting Rontonewtons to Nanonewtons

Rontonewton and Nanonewton both measure force, and the conversion matters whenever an SI-trained engineer needs to compare notes with a US-trained one, or vice versa. Specifically: Rontonewton is 1e-27 newton units, and nanonewton is 1e-9 newton units.

Formula

nanonewtons = rontonewtons × 1e-18

This factor comes from each unit's defined relationship to the category's base unit: 1 rontonewton equals 1e-27 base units, and 1 nanonewton equals 1e-9 base units, so dividing one by the other gives the direct rontonewton-to-nanonewton factor of 1e-18.

Simple example

1 rN × 1e-18 = 1e-18 nN

1 rontonewton = 1e-18 nanonewtons.
