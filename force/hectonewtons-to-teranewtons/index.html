About Converting Hectonewtons to Teranewtons

Hectonewton and Teranewton both measure force, and the conversion matters whenever an SI-trained engineer needs to compare notes with a US-trained one, or vice versa. Specifically: Hectonewton is 100 newton units, and teranewton is 1000000000000 newton units.

Formula

teranewtons = hectonewtons × 1e-10

This factor comes from each unit's defined relationship to the category's base unit: 1 hectonewton equals 100 base units, and 1 teranewton equals 1e+12 base units, so dividing one by the other gives the direct hectonewton-to-teranewton factor of 1e-10.

1 hN × 1e-10 = 1e-10 TN

1 hectonewton = 1e-10 teranewtons.
