About Converting Kilometer per Minutes to Nautical mile per Days

Kilometer per Minutes and Nautical mile per Days both describe speed, and the conversion matters whenever a maritime or aviation speed (knots) needs to be compared against a road speed (mph). Both are distance divided by time units.

Formula

nautical mile per days = kilometer per minutes × 777.537796976

This factor comes from each unit's defined relationship to the category's base unit: 1 kilometer per minutes equals 16.6666666667 base units, and 1 nautical mile per days equals 0.0214351851852 base units, so dividing one by the other gives the direct kilometer per minutes-to-nautical mile per days factor of 777.537796976.

Simple example

1 km/min × 777.537797 = 777.537797 nmi/d

1 kilometer per minutes = 777.537797 nautical mile per days.
