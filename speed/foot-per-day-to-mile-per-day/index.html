About Converting Foot per Days to Mile per Days

Foot per Days and Mile per Days both describe speed, and the conversion matters whenever a maritime or aviation speed (knots) needs to be compared against a road speed (mph). Both are distance divided by time units.

Formula

mile per days = foot per days × 0.000189393939394
