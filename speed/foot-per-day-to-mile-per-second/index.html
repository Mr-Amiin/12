About Converting Foot per Days to Mile per Seconds

Foot per Days and Mile per Seconds both describe speed, and the conversion matters whenever a maritime or aviation speed (knots) needs to be compared against a road speed (mph). Both are distance divided by time units.

Formula

mile per seconds = foot per days × 2.192059e-9

This factor comes from each unit's defined relationship to the category's base unit: 1 foot per days equals 0.00000352777777778 base units, and 1 mile per seconds equals 1609.344 base units, so dividing one by the other gives the direct foot per days-to-mile per seconds factor of 2.192059e-9.

Simple example

1 ft/d × 2.192059e-9 = 2.192059e-9 mi/s

1 foot per days = 2.192059e-9 mile per seconds.
