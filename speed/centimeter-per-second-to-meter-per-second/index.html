About Converting Centimeter per Seconds to m/s

Centimeter per Seconds and m/s both describe speed, and the conversion matters whenever a maritime or aviation speed (knots) needs to be compared against a road speed (mph). Specifically: Distance divided by time, and meters per second.

Formula

m/s = centimeter per seconds × 0.01

This factor comes from each unit's defined relationship to the category's base unit: 1 centimeter per seconds equals 0.01 base units, and 1 m/s equals 1 base unit, so dividing one by the other gives the direct centimeter per seconds-to-m/s factor of 0.01.

Simple example

1 cm/s × 0.01 = 0.01 m/s

1 centimeter per seconds = 0.01 m/s.

Real-world example

Understanding the Centimeter per Seconds (cm/s)

Distance divided by time.

Understanding the m/s

m/s (m/s) measures speed. The meter per second is the SI derived unit of speed: one meter of distance per second of time. It is classified as a SI derived unit.

Where it's used: Standard scientific speed unit worldwide

Uses of the m/s today

  • Scientific and engineering speed/velocity calculations
  • Meteorology (wind speed, alongside km/h and knots)
  • Physics education and calculations

History of the m/s

As a coherent SI derived unit, meters per second follows automatically from the meter and second, the SI base units of length and time, without an independent historical origin.

Historical origin. m/s was not the invention of a single named person; it developed through the process described above.
