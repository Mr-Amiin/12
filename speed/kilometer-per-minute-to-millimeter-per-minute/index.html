About Converting Kilometer per Minutes to Millimeter per Minutes

Kilometer per Minutes and Millimeter per Minutes both describe speed, and the conversion matters whenever a maritime or aviation speed (knots) needs to be compared against a road speed (mph). Both are distance divided by time units.

Formula

millimeter per minutes = kilometer per minutes × 1000000

This factor comes from each unit's defined relationship to the category's base unit: 1 kilometer per minutes equals 16.6666666667 base units, and 1 millimeter per minutes equals 0.0000166666666667 base units, so dividing one by the other gives the direct kilometer per minutes-to-millimeter per minutes factor of 1000000.

Simple example

1 km/min × 1000000 = 1,000,000 mm/min

1 kilometer per minutes = 1,000,000 millimeter per minutes.

Real-world example

10 km/min × 1000000 = 10,000,000 mm/min

10 kilometer per minutes = 10,000,000 millimeter per minutes.
