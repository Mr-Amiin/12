About Converting Nautical mile per Hours to Millimeter per Seconds

Nautical mile per Hours and Millimeter per Seconds both describe speed, and the conversion matters whenever a maritime or aviation speed (knots) needs to be compared against a road speed (mph). Both are distance divided by time units.

Formula

millimeter per seconds = nautical mile per hours × 514.444444444

This factor comes from each unit's defined relationship to the category's base unit: 1 nautical mile per hours equals 0.514444444444 base units, and 1 millimeter per seconds equals 0.001 base units, so dividing one by the other gives the direct nautical mile per hours-to-millimeter per seconds factor of 514.444444444.

1 nmi/h × 514.4444444 = 514.4444444 mm/s
