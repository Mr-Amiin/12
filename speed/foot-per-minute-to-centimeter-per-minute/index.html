About Converting Foot per Minutes to Centimeter per Minutes

Foot per Minutes and Centimeter per Minutes both describe speed, and the conversion matters whenever a maritime or aviation speed (knots) needs to be compared against a road speed (mph). Both are distance divided by time units.

Formula

centimeter per minutes = foot per minutes × 30.48

This factor comes from each unit's defined relationship to the category's base unit: 1 foot per minutes equals 0.00508 base units, and 1 centimeter per minutes equals 0.000166666666667 base units, so dividing one by the other gives the direct foot per minutes-to-centimeter per minutes factor of 30.48.

Simple example

1 ft/min × 30.48 = 30.48 cm/min

1 foot per minutes = 30.48 centimeter per minutes.

Real-world example

10 ft/min × 30.48 = 304.8 cm/min

10 foot per minutes = 304.8 centimeter per minutes.
