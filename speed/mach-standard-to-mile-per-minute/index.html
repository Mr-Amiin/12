About Converting Maches to Mile per Minutes

Mach and Mile per Minutes both describe speed, and the conversion matters whenever a maritime or aviation speed (knots) needs to be compared against a road speed (mph). Specifically: Mach at standard sea-level conditions, and distance divided by time.

Formula

mile per minutes = maches × 12.6867841804

This factor comes from each unit's defined relationship to the category's base unit: 1 mach equals 340.29 base units, and 1 mile per minutes equals 26.8224 base units, so dividing one by the other gives the direct mach-to-mile per minutes factor of 12.6867841804.
