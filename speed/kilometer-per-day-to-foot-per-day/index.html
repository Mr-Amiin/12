About Converting Kilometer per Days to Foot per Days

Kilometer per Days and Foot per Days both describe speed, and the conversion matters whenever a maritime or aviation speed (knots) needs to be compared against a road speed (mph). Both are distance divided by time units.

Formula

foot per days = kilometer per days × 3280.83989501

This factor comes from each unit's defined relationship to the category's base unit: 1 kilometer per days equals 0.0115740740741 base units, and 1 foot per days equals 0.00000352777777778 base units, so dividing one by the other gives the direct kilometer per days-to-foot per days factor of 3280.83989501.
