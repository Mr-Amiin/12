About Converting Speed of lights to Yard per Hours

Speed of light and Yard per Hours both describe speed, and the conversion matters whenever a maritime or aviation speed (knots) needs to be compared against a road speed (mph). Specifically: Speed of light in vacuum, and distance divided by time.

Formula

yard per hours = speed of lights × 1.180285e+12

This factor comes from each unit's defined relationship to the category's base unit: 1 speed of light equals 299792458 base units, and 1 yard per hours equals 0.000254 base units, so dividing one by the other gives the direct speed of light-to-yard per hours factor of 1.180285e+12.
