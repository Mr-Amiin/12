Understanding the mph

mph (mph) measures speed. Miles per hour follows directly from the international statute mile (1,609.344 m) divided by one hour. It is classified as a US/imperial customary unit.

Where it's used: United States (road speed limits and vehicle speedometers) United Kingdom (road speed limits)

Uses of the mph today

  • Road speed limits and vehicle speedometers in the US and UK
  • Weather reporting (wind speed) in the US
  • Sports statistics (e.g. pitch speed, wind gusts) in US media

History of the mph

Miles per hour is a compound unit with no separate origin story of its own — it follows automatically once the statute mile was fixed at 5,280 feet in 1593 and later refined to exactly 1,609.344 meters by the 1959 international agreement that also standardized the pound.

Historical origin. mph was not the invention of a single named person; it developed through the process described above.
