About Converting Centimeter per Minutes to Foot per Hours

Centimeter per Minutes and Foot per Hours both describe speed, and the conversion matters whenever a maritime or aviation speed (knots) needs to be compared against a road speed (mph). Both are distance divided by time units.

Formula

foot per hours = centimeter per minutes × 1.96850393701
