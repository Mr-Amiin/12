About Converting Yard per Hours to Meter per Days

Yard per Hours and Meter per Days both describe speed, and the conversion matters whenever a maritime or aviation speed (knots) needs to be compared against a road speed (mph). Both are distance divided by time units.

Formula

meter per days = yard per hours × 21.9456

This factor comes from each unit's defined relationship to the category's base unit: 1 yard per hours equals 0.000254 base units, and 1 meter per days equals 0.0000115740740741 base units, so dividing one by the other gives the direct yard per hours-to-meter per days factor of 21.9456.

Simple example

1 yd/h × 21.9456 = 21.9456 m/d

1 yard per hours = 21.9456 meter per days.

Real-world example

10 yd/h × 21.9456 = 219.456 m/d

10 yard per hours = 219.456 meter per days.
