About Converting Millimeter per Seconds to Meter per Days

Millimeter per Seconds and Meter per Days both describe speed, and the conversion matters whenever a maritime or aviation speed (knots) needs to be compared against a road speed (mph). Both are distance divided by time units.

Formula

meter per days = millimeter per seconds × 86.4

This factor comes from each unit's defined relationship to the category's base unit: 1 millimeter per seconds equals 0.001 base units, and 1 meter per days equals 0.0000115740740741 base units, so dividing one by the other gives the direct millimeter per seconds-to-meter per days factor of 86.4.

Simple example

1 mm/s × 86.4 = 86.4 m/d

1 millimeter per seconds = 86.4 meter per days.

Real-world example

10 mm/s × 86.4 = 864 m/d

10 millimeter per seconds = 864 meter per days.
