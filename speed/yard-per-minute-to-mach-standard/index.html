About Converting Yard per Minutes to Maches

Yard per Minutes and Mach both describe speed, and the conversion matters whenever a maritime or aviation speed (knots) needs to be compared against a road speed (mph). Specifically: Distance divided by time, and mach at standard sea-level conditions.
