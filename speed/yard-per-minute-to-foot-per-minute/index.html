About Converting Yard per Minutes to Foot per Minutes

Yard per Minutes and Foot per Minutes both describe speed, and the conversion matters whenever a maritime or aviation speed (knots) needs to be compared against a road speed (mph). Both are distance divided by time units.

Formula

foot per minutes = yard per minutes × 3

This factor comes from each unit's defined relationship to the category's base unit: 1 yard per minutes equals 0.01524 base units, and 1 foot per minutes equals 0.00508 base units, so dividing one by the other gives the direct yard per minutes-to-foot per minutes factor of 3.

Simple example

1 yd/min × 3 = 3 ft/min

1 yard per minutes = 3 foot per minutes.

Real-world example

10 yd/min × 3 = 30 ft/min

10 yard per minutes = 30 foot per minutes.
