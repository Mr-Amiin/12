About Converting ft/s to Foot per Hours

ft/s and Foot per Hours both describe speed, and the conversion matters whenever a maritime or aviation speed (knots) needs to be compared against a road speed (mph). Specifically: Feet per second, and distance divided by time.

Formula

foot per hours = ft/s × 3600

This factor comes from each unit's defined relationship to the category's base unit: 1 ft/s equals 0.3048 base units, and 1 foot per hours equals 0.0000846666666667 base units, so dividing one by the other gives the direct ft/s-to-foot per hours factor of 3600.

Simple example

1 ft/s × 3600 = 3,600 ft/h

1 ft/s = 3,600 foot per hours.

Real-world example

10 ft/s × 3600 = 36,000 ft/h

10 ft/s = 36,000 foot per hours.
