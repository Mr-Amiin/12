About Converting ft/s to Centimeter per Hours

ft/s and Centimeter per Hours both describe speed, and the conversion matters whenever a maritime or aviation speed (knots) needs to be compared against a road speed (mph). Specifically: Feet per second, and distance divided by time.

Formula

centimeter per hours = ft/s × 109728

This factor comes from each unit's defined relationship to the category's base unit: 1 ft/s equals 0.3048 base units, and 1 centimeter per hours equals 0.00000277777777778 base units, so dividing one by the other gives the direct ft/s-to-centimeter per hours factor of 109728.

Simple example

1 ft/s × 109728 = 109,728 cm/h

1 ft/s = 109,728 centimeter per hours.

Real-world example

10 ft/s × 109728 = 1,097,280 cm/h

10 ft/s = 1,097,280 centimeter per hours.
