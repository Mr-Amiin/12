About Converting Maches to Kilometer per Seconds

Mach and Kilometer per Seconds both describe speed, and the conversion matters whenever a maritime or aviation speed (knots) needs to be compared against a road speed (mph). Specifically: Mach at standard sea-level conditions, and distance divided by time.

Formula

kilometer per seconds = maches × 0.34029

This factor comes from each unit's defined relationship to the category's base unit: 1 mach equals 340.29 base units, and 1 kilometer per seconds equals 1000 base units, so dividing one by the other gives the direct mach-to-kilometer per seconds factor of 0.34029.

Simple example

1 Ma × 0.34029 = 0.34029 km/s

1 mach = 0.34029 kilometer per seconds.

Real-world example

10 Ma × 0.34029 = 3.4029 km/s

10 maches = 3.4029 kilometer per seconds.
