Understanding the Meter per Hours (m/h)

Distance divided by time.

Understanding the m/s

m/s (m/s) measures speed. The meter per second is the SI derived unit of speed: one meter of distance per second of time. It is classified as a SI derived unit.

Where it's used: Standard scientific speed unit worldwide

Uses of the m/s today

  • Scientific and engineering speed/velocity calculations
  • Meteorology (wind speed, alongside km/h and knots)
  • Physics education and calculations

History of the m/s

As a coherent SI derived unit, meters per second follows automatically from the meter and second, the SI base units of length and time, without an independent historical origin.

Historical origin. m/s was not the invention of a single named person; it developed through the process described above.
