About Converting km/h to Centimeter per Seconds

km/h and Centimeter per Seconds both describe speed, and the conversion matters whenever a maritime or aviation speed (knots) needs to be compared against a road speed (mph). km/h is most often used for road speed limits and vehicle speedometers outside the us.

Formula

centimeter per seconds = km/h × 27.7777777778

This factor comes from each unit's defined relationship to the category's base unit: 1 km/h equals 0.277777777778 base units, and 1 centimeter per seconds equals 0.01 base units, so dividing one by the other gives the direct km/h-to-centimeter per seconds factor of 27.7777777778.

Understanding the km/h

km/h (km/h) measures speed. Kilometers per hour follows directly from the kilometer divided by one hour. It is classified as a SI-derived (metric) unit.

Where it's used: Standard road-speed unit worldwide except the US

Uses of the km/h today

  • Road speed limits and vehicle speedometers outside the US
  • Weather reporting (wind speed) in most countries

History of the km/h

Km/h is a compound unit with no independent origin story — it follows automatically once the kilometer and hour were each standardized, becoming the default speed unit as metric countries motorized through the 20th century.

Historical origin. km/h was not the invention of a single named person; it developed through the process described above.
