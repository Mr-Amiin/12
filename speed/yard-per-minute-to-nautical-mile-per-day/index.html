About Converting Yard per Minutes to Nautical mile per Days

Yard per Minutes and Nautical mile per Days both describe speed, and the conversion matters whenever a maritime or aviation speed (knots) needs to be compared against a road speed (mph). Both are distance divided by time units.

Formula

nautical mile per days = yard per minutes × 0.710980561555

This factor comes from each unit's defined relationship to the category's base unit: 1 yard per minutes equals 0.01524 base units, and 1 nautical mile per days equals 0.0214351851852 base units, so dividing one by the other gives the direct yard per minutes-to-nautical mile per days factor of 0.710980561555.
