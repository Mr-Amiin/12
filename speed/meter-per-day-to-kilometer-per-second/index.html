About Converting Meter per Days to Kilometer per Seconds

Meter per Days and Kilometer per Seconds both describe speed, and the conversion matters whenever a maritime or aviation speed (knots) needs to be compared against a road speed (mph). Both are distance divided by time units.

Formula

kilometer per seconds = meter per days × 1.157407e-8

This factor comes from each unit's defined relationship to the category's base unit: 1 meter per days equals 0.0000115740740741 base units, and 1 kilometer per seconds equals 1000 base units, so dividing one by the other gives the direct meter per days-to-kilometer per seconds factor of 1.157407e-8.

Simple example

1 m/d × 1.157407e-8 = 1.157407e-8 km/s

1 meter per days = 1.157407e-8 kilometer per seconds.

Real-world example
