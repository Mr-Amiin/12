About Converting Nautical mile per Minutes to Foot per Hours

Nautical mile per Minutes and Foot per Hours both describe speed, and the conversion matters whenever a maritime or aviation speed (knots) needs to be compared against a road speed (mph). Both are distance divided by time units.

Formula

foot per hours = nautical mile per minutes × 364566.929134

This factor comes from each unit's defined relationship to the category's base unit: 1 nautical mile per minutes equals 30.8666666667 base units, and 1 foot per hours equals 0.0000846666666667 base units, so dividing one by the other gives the direct nautical mile per minutes-to-foot per hours factor of 364566.929134.
