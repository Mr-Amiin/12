About Converting Picojoules to Terajoules

Picojoule and Terajoule both measure a quantity used in structural or mechanical engineering — commonly stress, pressure, force, torque, power, or energy — where converting between the unit conventions used in different regions, industries, or equipment specifications is a routine part of design work. Specifically: Picojoule is 1e-12 joule units, and terajoule is 1000000000000 joule units.

Formula

terajoules = picojoules × 1e-24

This factor comes from each unit's defined relationship to the category's base unit: 1 picojoule equals 1e-12 base units, and 1 terajoule equals 1e+12 base units, so dividing one by the other gives the direct picojoule-to-terajoule factor of 1e-24.

Simple example

1 pJ × 1e-24 = 1e-24 TJ

1 picojoule = 1e-24 terajoules.

Is the picojoule to terajoule conversion exact?

Yes. Both picojoule and terajoule are defined by fixed standards rather than physical artifacts, so the factor of 1e-24 used above is exact to as many digits as you choose to display.

Why do engineering unit conventions vary so much by region and industry?

Pressure, force, and stress can be expressed in metric (pascal, newton), US customary (psi, pound-force), or older and industry-specific units (atmosphere, torr, kip) depending on the country, industry, and even the equipment manufacturer, which is why engineers routinely need to convert between them when working across specs from different sources.
