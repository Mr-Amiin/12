About Converting Rontojoules to Ergs

Rontojoule and Erg both measure a quantity used in structural or mechanical engineering — commonly stress, pressure, force, torque, power, or energy — where converting between the unit conventions used in different regions, industries, or equipment specifications is a routine part of design work. Specifically: Rontojoule is 1e-27 joule units, and an erg is a CGS energy unit.

Formula

ergs = rontojoules × 1e-20

This factor comes from each unit's defined relationship to the category's base unit: 1 rontojoule equals 1e-27 base units, and 1 erg equals 1e-7 base units, so dividing one by the other gives the direct rontojoule-to-erg factor of 1e-20.

Why do engineering unit conventions vary so much by region and industry?

Pressure, force, and stress can be expressed in metric (pascal, newton), US customary (psi, pound-force), or older and industry-specific units (atmosphere, torr, kip) depending on the country, industry, and even the equipment manufacturer, which is why engineers routinely need to convert between them when working across specs from different sources.
