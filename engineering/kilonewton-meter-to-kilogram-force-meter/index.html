Understanding the Kilonewton meter (kN m)

Understanding the Kilogram-force meter

Kilogram-force meter (kgf m) measures torque. The torque produced by one kilogram-force acting at a perpendicular distance of one meter. It is classified as a non-SI unit, deprecated but still encountered.

Where it's used: Can be encountered in some older European and Japanese automotive and mechanical torque specifications

kgf·m vs. N·m. The newton-meter is the modern SI torque unit; kilogram-force meter is the older, gravity-dependent equivalent still occasionally seen on legacy specifications. 1 kgf·m equals exactly 9.80665 N·m.

Uses of the Kilogram-force meter today

  • Appears on some legacy torque specifications and equipment predating wider adoption of the newton-meter

Kilogram-force meter — sources

  • BIPM SI Brochure — Kilogram-force and its derivatives are non-SI units whose use is discouraged in favor of SI force/torque units.
