About Converting Micrograms to Gigagrams

Weight conversions between Microgram and Gigagram come up constantly in everyday commerce, shipping, and cooking, since US packaging is usually labeled in pounds while most of the rest of the world uses kilograms. Specifically: Microgram is 0.000001 gram units, and gigagram is 1000000000 gram units.

Formula

gigagrams = micrograms × 1e-15

This factor comes from each unit's defined relationship to the category's base unit: 1 microgram equals 1e-9 base units, and 1 gigagram equals 1000000 base units, so dividing one by the other gives the direct microgram-to-gigagram factor of 1e-15.
