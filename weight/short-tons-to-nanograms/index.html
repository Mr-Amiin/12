About Converting Short tons to Nanograms

Weight conversions between Short ton and Nanogram come up constantly in everyday commerce, shipping, and cooking, since US packaging is usually labeled in pounds while most of the rest of the world uses kilograms. Specifically: A US short ton is 2,000 pounds, and nanogram is 1e-9 gram units.
