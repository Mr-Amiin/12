About Converting Decagrams to Milligrams

Weight conversions between Decagram and Milligram come up constantly in everyday commerce, shipping, and cooking, since US packaging is usually labeled in pounds while most of the rest of the world uses kilograms. Specifically: Decagram is 10 gram units, and milligram is 0.001 gram units.

Formula

milligrams = decagrams × 10000

This factor comes from each unit's defined relationship to the category's base unit: 1 decagram equals 0.01 base units, and 1 milligram equals 0.000001 base units, so dividing one by the other gives the direct decagram-to-milligram factor of 10000.
