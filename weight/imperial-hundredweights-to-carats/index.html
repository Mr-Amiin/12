About Converting Imperial hundredweights to Carats

Weight conversions between Imperial hundredweight and Carat come up constantly in everyday commerce, shipping, and cooking, since US packaging is usually labeled in pounds while most of the rest of the world uses kilograms. Specifically: An imperial long hundredweight is 112 pounds, and a metric carat is exactly 200 milligrams.

carats = imperial hundredweights × 254011.7272

This factor comes from each unit's defined relationship to the category's base unit: 1 imperial hundredweight equals 50.80234544 base units, and 1 carat equals 0.0002 base units, so dividing one by the other gives the direct imperial hundredweight-to-carat factor of 254011.7272.
