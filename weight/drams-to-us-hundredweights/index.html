About Converting Drams to US hundredweights

Weight conversions between Dram and US hundredweight come up constantly in everyday commerce, shipping, and cooking, since US packaging is usually labeled in pounds while most of the rest of the world uses kilograms. Specifically: An avoirdupois dram is 1/16 ounce, and a US short hundredweight is 100 pounds.
