About Converting Nanohertz to Decahertz

Specifically: Nanohertz is 1e-9 hertz units, and decahertz is 10 hertz units.

Formula

decahertz = nanohertz × 1e-10

This factor comes from each unit's defined relationship to the category's base unit: 1 nanohertz equals 1e-9 base units, and 1 decahertz equals 10 base units, so dividing one by the other gives the direct nanohertz-to-decahertz factor of 1e-10.
