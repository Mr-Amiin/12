Understanding the New Zealand Dollar

New Zealand Dollar ($) measures currency. NZD is the official currency of New Zealand; its value floats on foreign exchange markets. It is classified as a ISO 4217 currency code.

Where it's used: New Zealand and several Pacific island nations

Uses of the New Zealand Dollar today

  • Domestic New Zealand transactions
  • Agricultural export trade (dairy, meat)
  • Regional Pacific trade

History of the New Zealand Dollar

New Zealand introduced its dollar in 1967, replacing the New Zealand pound as part of decimalization, following a path similar to Australia's the previous year.

Historical origin. New Zealand Dollar was not the invention of a single named person; it developed through the process described above.
