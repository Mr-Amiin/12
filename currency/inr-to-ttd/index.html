Understanding the Indian Rupee

Indian Rupee (₹) measures currency. INR is the official currency of India; its value floats on foreign exchange markets, with periodic central bank intervention. It is classified as a ISO 4217 currency code.

Where it's used: India

Uses of the Indian Rupee today

  • Domestic Indian transactions
  • One of the largest remittance corridors globally (from USD/GBP-earning expatriates)
  • Regional South Asian trade

History of the Indian Rupee

The modern rupee's name derives from the Sanskrit 'rupya' (silver coin), with a continuous history as India's currency stretching back centuries; the current decimal system (100 paise to the rupee) was adopted in 1957.

Historical origin. Indian Rupee was not the invention of a single named person; it developed through the process described above.

Understanding the Trinidad and Tobago Dollar ($)

The Trinidad and Tobago Dollar (TTD) is the official currency of Trinidad and Tobago.
