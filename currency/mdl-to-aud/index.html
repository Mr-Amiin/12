Understanding the Australian Dollar

Australian Dollar ($) measures currency. AUD is the official currency of Australia; its value floats on foreign exchange markets. It is classified as a ISO 4217 currency code.

Where it's used: Australia and several Pacific island nations

Uses of the Australian Dollar today

  • Domestic Australian transactions
  • Commodity-linked trading (AUD often correlates with mining/resource exports)
  • Regional trade with Asia-Pacific

History of the Australian Dollar

Australia introduced the Australian dollar in 1966, replacing the Australian pound as part of a broader decimalization of its currency system.

Historical origin. Australian Dollar was not the invention of a single named person; it developed through the process described above.
