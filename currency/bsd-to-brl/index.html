Understanding the Brazilian Real

Brazilian Real (R$) measures currency. BRL is the official currency of Brazil; its value floats on foreign exchange markets. It is classified as a ISO 4217 currency code.

Where it's used: Brazil

Uses of the Brazilian Real today

  • Domestic Brazilian transactions
  • Largest economy in South America's trade and finance
  • Commodity-linked trading

History of the Brazilian Real

The real was introduced in 1994 as the centerpiece of the 'Plano Real' economic stabilization program, which successfully ended a period of extreme hyperinflation in Brazil.

Historical origin. Brazilian Real was not the invention of a single named person; it developed through the process described above.
