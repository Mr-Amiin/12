Understanding the Mexican Peso

Mexican Peso ($) measures currency. MXN is the official currency of Mexico; its value floats on foreign exchange markets. It is classified as a ISO 4217 currency code.

Where it's used: Mexico

Uses of the Mexican Peso today

  • Domestic Mexican transactions
  • Major US-Mexico trade and remittance corridor
  • Tourism

History of the Mexican Peso

The modern Mexican peso was introduced in 1993 ('nuevo peso') at a rate of 1,000 old pesos, a revaluation addressing decades of inflation, before dropping the 'nuevo' label in 1996.

Historical origin. Mexican Peso was not the invention of a single named person; it developed through the process described above.

Understanding the Hong Kong Dollar ($)

The Hong Kong Dollar (HKD) is the official currency of Hong Kong.
