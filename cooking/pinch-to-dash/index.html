About Converting Pinches to Dashs

Pinch and Dash both show up in recipes, and converting between them matters whenever a recipe from one country's cookbook needs to be followed with another country's measuring tools. Specifically: A cooking pinch approximated as 1/16 teaspoon, and a cooking dash approximated as 1/8 teaspoon.

Formula

dashs = pinches × 0.5

This factor comes from each unit's defined relationship to the category's base unit: 1 pinch equals 3.080576e-7 base units, and 1 dash equals 6.161152e-7 base units, so dividing one by the other gives the direct pinch-to-dash factor of 0.5.

Simple example

1 pinch × 0.5 = 0.5 dash

1 pinch = 0.5 dashs.

Real-world example

2 pinch × 0.5 = 1 dash

2 pinches = 1 dash.
