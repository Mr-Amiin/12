About Converting Tablespoons to Pinches

Tablespoon and Pinch both show up in recipes, and converting between them matters whenever a recipe from one country's cookbook needs to be followed with another country's measuring tools. Tablespoon is most often used for us recipe and cooking measurement.

Formula

pinches = tablespoons × 47.9999999391

This factor comes from each unit's defined relationship to the category's base unit: 1 tablespoon equals 0.0000147867647813 base units, and 1 pinch equals 3.080576e-7 base units, so dividing one by the other gives the direct tablespoon-to-pinch factor of 47.9999999391.

Understanding the Tablespoon

Tablespoon (tbsp) measures volume. The US tablespoon is exactly 14.78676478125 milliliters — 3 US teaspoons, or 1/16 of a US cup. It is classified as a US customary unit.
