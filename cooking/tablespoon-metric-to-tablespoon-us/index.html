About Converting Metric tablespoons to Tablespoons

Metric tablespoon and Tablespoon both show up in recipes, and converting between them matters whenever a recipe from one country's cookbook needs to be followed with another country's measuring tools. Specifically: A metric tablespoon is 15 milliliters, and a US tablespoon is half a US fluid ounce.

Formula

tablespoons = metric tablespoons × 1.01442068106

Understanding the Tablespoon

Tablespoon (tbsp) measures volume. The US tablespoon is exactly 14.78676478125 milliliters — 3 US teaspoons, or 1/16 of a US cup. It is classified as a US customary unit.
