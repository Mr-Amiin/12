About Converting Metric tablespoons to Teaspoons

Metric tablespoon and Teaspoon both show up in recipes, and converting between them matters whenever a recipe from one country's cookbook needs to be followed with another country's measuring tools. Specifically: A metric tablespoon is 15 milliliters, and a US teaspoon is one third of a US tablespoon.

Formula

teaspoons = metric tablespoons × 3.04326204317

This factor comes from each unit's defined relationship to the category's base unit: 1 metric tablespoon equals 0.000015 base units, and 1 teaspoon equals 0.00000492892159375 base units, so dividing one by the other gives the direct metric tablespoon-to-teaspoon factor of 3.04326204317.

Understanding the Teaspoon

Teaspoon (tsp) measures volume. The US teaspoon is exactly 4.92892159375 milliliters — 1/3 of a US tablespoon and 1/48 of a US cup. It is classified as a US customary unit.

Where it's used: United States (cooking and recipe measurement)

Uses of the Teaspoon today

  • US recipe and cooking measurement for small quantities
  • Medication dosing instructions in the US
  • Baking measurement alongside the tablespoon and cup

Teaspoon — sources
