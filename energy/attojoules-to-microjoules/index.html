About Converting Attojoules to Microjoules

Attojoule and Microjoule both measure energy, and the conversion is especially useful for comparing nutrition labels (kilocalories) against scientific energy figures (joules). Specifically: Attojoule is 1e-18 joule units, and microjoule is 0.000001 joule units.

Formula

microjoules = attojoules × 1e-12

This factor comes from each unit's defined relationship to the category's base unit: 1 attojoule equals 1e-18 base units, and 1 microjoule equals 0.000001 base units, so dividing one by the other gives the direct attojoule-to-microjoule factor of 1e-12.

Simple example

1 aJ × 1e-12 = 1e-12 uJ

1 attojoule = 1e-12 microjoules.

Real-world example

100 aJ × 1e-12 = 1e-10 uJ

100 attojoules = 1e-10 microjoules.
