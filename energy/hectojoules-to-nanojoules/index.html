About Converting Hectojoules to Nanojoules

Hectojoule and Nanojoule both measure energy, and the conversion is especially useful for comparing nutrition labels (kilocalories) against scientific energy figures (joules). Specifically: Hectojoule is 100 joule units, and nanojoule is 1e-9 joule units.

Formula

nanojoules = hectojoules × 100000000000

This factor comes from each unit's defined relationship to the category's base unit: 1 hectojoule equals 100 base units, and 1 nanojoule equals 1e-9 base units, so dividing one by the other gives the direct hectojoule-to-nanojoule factor of 100000000000.

Simple example

1 hJ × 100000000000 = 100,000,000,000 nJ

1 hectojoule = 100,000,000,000 nanojoules.

Real-world example

100 hJ × 100000000000 = 1e+13 nJ

100 hectojoules = 1e+13 nanojoules.
