About Converting Petajoules to Attojoules

Petajoule and Attojoule both measure energy, and the conversion is especially useful for comparing nutrition labels (kilocalories) against scientific energy figures (joules). Specifically: Petajoule is 1000000000000000 joule units, and attojoule is 1e-18 joule units.

Formula

attojoules = petajoules × 1e+33

This factor comes from each unit's defined relationship to the category's base unit: 1 petajoule equals 1e+15 base units, and 1 attojoule equals 1e-18 base units, so dividing one by the other gives the direct petajoule-to-attojoule factor of 1e+33.

Simple example

1 PJ × 1e+33 = 1e+33 aJ

1 petajoule = 1e+33 attojoules.

Real-world example

100 PJ × 1e+33 = 1e+35 aJ

100 petajoules = 1e+35 attojoules.
