About Converting Decajoules to Kilojoules

Decajoule and Kilojoule both measure energy, and the conversion is especially useful for comparing nutrition labels (kilocalories) against scientific energy figures (joules). Specifically: Decajoule is 10 joule units, and kilojoule is 1000 joule units.

Formula

kilojoules = decajoules × 0.01

This factor comes from each unit's defined relationship to the category's base unit: 1 decajoule equals 10 base units, and 1 kilojoule equals 1000 base units, so dividing one by the other gives the direct decajoule-to-kilojoule factor of 0.01.

Simple example

1 daJ × 0.01 = 0.01 kJ

1 decajoule = 0.01 kilojoules.

Real-world example

100 daJ × 0.01 = 1 kJ

100 decajoules = 1 kilojoule.
