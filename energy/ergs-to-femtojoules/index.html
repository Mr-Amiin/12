About Converting Ergs to Femtojoules

Erg and Femtojoule both measure energy, and the conversion is especially useful for comparing nutrition labels (kilocalories) against scientific energy figures (joules). Specifically: An erg is a CGS energy unit, and femtojoule is 1e-15 joule units.

Formula

femtojoules = ergs × 100000000

This factor comes from each unit's defined relationship to the category's base unit: 1 erg equals 1e-7 base units, and 1 femtojoule equals 1e-15 base units, so dividing one by the other gives the direct erg-to-femtojoule factor of 100000000.

Simple example

1 erg × 100000000 = 100,000,000 fJ

1 erg = 100,000,000 femtojoules.

Real-world example

100 erg × 100000000 = 10,000,000,000 fJ

100 ergs = 10,000,000,000 femtojoules.
