About Converting Microjoules to Decajoules

Microjoule and Decajoule both measure energy, and the conversion is especially useful for comparing nutrition labels (kilocalories) against scientific energy figures (joules). Specifically: Microjoule is 0.000001 joule units, and decajoule is 10 joule units.

Formula

decajoules = microjoules × 1e-7

This factor comes from each unit's defined relationship to the category's base unit: 1 microjoule equals 0.000001 base units, and 1 decajoule equals 10 base units, so dividing one by the other gives the direct microjoule-to-decajoule factor of 1e-7.

Simple example

1 uJ × 1e-7 = 1e-7 daJ

1 microjoule = 1e-7 decajoules.

Real-world example

100 uJ × 1e-7 = 0.00001 daJ

100 microjoules = 0.00001 decajoules.
