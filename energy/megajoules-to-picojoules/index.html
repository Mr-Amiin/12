About Converting Megajoules to Picojoules

Megajoule and Picojoule both measure energy, and the conversion is especially useful for comparing nutrition labels (kilocalories) against scientific energy figures (joules). Specifically: Megajoule is 1000000 joule units, and picojoule is 1e-12 joule units.

Formula

picojoules = megajoules × 1e+18

This factor comes from each unit's defined relationship to the category's base unit: 1 megajoule equals 1000000 base units, and 1 picojoule equals 1e-12 base units, so dividing one by the other gives the direct megajoule-to-picojoule factor of 1e+18.

Simple example

1 MJ × 1e+18 = 1e+18 pJ

1 megajoule = 1e+18 picojoules.

Real-world example

100 MJ × 1e+18 = 1e+20 pJ

100 megajoules = 1e+20 picojoules.
