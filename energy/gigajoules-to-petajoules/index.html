About Converting Gigajoules to Petajoules

Gigajoule and Petajoule both measure energy, and the conversion is especially useful for comparing nutrition labels (kilocalories) against scientific energy figures (joules). Specifically: Gigajoule is 1000000000 joule units, and petajoule is 1000000000000000 joule units.

Formula

petajoules = gigajoules × 0.000001

This factor comes from each unit's defined relationship to the category's base unit: 1 gigajoule equals 1000000000 base units, and 1 petajoule equals 1e+15 base units, so dividing one by the other gives the direct gigajoule-to-petajoule factor of 0.000001.

Simple example

1 GJ × 0.000001 = 0.000001 PJ

1 gigajoule = 0.000001 petajoules.

Real-world example

100 GJ × 0.000001 = 0.0001 PJ

100 gigajoules = 0.0001 petajoules.
