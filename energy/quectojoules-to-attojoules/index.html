About Converting Quectojoules to Attojoules

Quectojoule and Attojoule both measure energy, and the conversion is especially useful for comparing nutrition labels (kilocalories) against scientific energy figures (joules). Specifically: Quectojoule is 1e-30 joule units, and attojoule is 1e-18 joule units.

Formula

attojoules = quectojoules × 1e-12

This factor comes from each unit's defined relationship to the category's base unit: 1 quectojoule equals 1e-30 base units, and 1 attojoule equals 1e-18 base units, so dividing one by the other gives the direct quectojoule-to-attojoule factor of 1e-12.

Simple example

1 qJ × 1e-12 = 1e-12 aJ

1 quectojoule = 1e-12 attojoules.

Real-world example

100 qJ × 1e-12 = 1e-10 aJ

100 quectojoules = 1e-10 attojoules.
