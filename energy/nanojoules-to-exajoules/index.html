About Converting Nanojoules to Exajoules

Nanojoule and Exajoule both measure energy, and the conversion is especially useful for comparing nutrition labels (kilocalories) against scientific energy figures (joules). Specifically: Nanojoule is 1e-9 joule units, and exajoule is 1000000000000000000 joule units.

Formula

exajoules = nanojoules × 1e-27

This factor comes from each unit's defined relationship to the category's base unit: 1 nanojoule equals 1e-9 base units, and 1 exajoule equals 1e+18 base units, so dividing one by the other gives the direct nanojoule-to-exajoule factor of 1e-27.

Simple example

1 nJ × 1e-27 = 1e-27 EJ

1 nanojoule = 1e-27 exajoules.

Real-world example

100 nJ × 1e-27 = 1e-25 EJ

100 nanojoules = 1e-25 exajoules.
