About Converting Exajoules to Millijoules

Exajoule and Millijoule both measure energy, and the conversion is especially useful for comparing nutrition labels (kilocalories) against scientific energy figures (joules). Specifically: Exajoule is 1000000000000000000 joule units, and millijoule is 0.001 joule units.

Formula

millijoules = exajoules × 1e+21

This factor comes from each unit's defined relationship to the category's base unit: 1 exajoule equals 1e+18 base units, and 1 millijoule equals 0.001 base units, so dividing one by the other gives the direct exajoule-to-millijoule factor of 1e+21.

Simple example

1 EJ × 1e+21 = 1e+21 mJ

1 exajoule = 1e+21 millijoules.

Real-world example

100 EJ × 1e+21 = 1e+23 mJ

100 exajoules = 1e+23 millijoules.
