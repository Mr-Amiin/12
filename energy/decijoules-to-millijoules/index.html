About Converting Decijoules to Millijoules

Decijoule and Millijoule both measure energy, and the conversion is especially useful for comparing nutrition labels (kilocalories) against scientific energy figures (joules). Specifically: Decijoule is 0.1 joule units, and millijoule is 0.001 joule units.

Formula

millijoules = decijoules × 100

This factor comes from each unit's defined relationship to the category's base unit: 1 decijoule equals 0.1 base units, and 1 millijoule equals 0.001 base units, so dividing one by the other gives the direct decijoule-to-millijoule factor of 100.

Simple example

1 dJ × 100 = 100 mJ

1 decijoule = 100 millijoules.

Real-world example

100 dJ × 100 = 10,000 mJ

100 decijoules = 10,000 millijoules.
