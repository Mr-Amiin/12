About Converting Decajoules to Zeptojoules

Decajoule and Zeptojoule both measure energy, and the conversion is especially useful for comparing nutrition labels (kilocalories) against scientific energy figures (joules). Specifically: Decajoule is 10 joule units, and zeptojoule is 1e-21 joule units.

Formula

zeptojoules = decajoules × 1e+22

This factor comes from each unit's defined relationship to the category's base unit: 1 decajoule equals 10 base units, and 1 zeptojoule equals 1e-21 base units, so dividing one by the other gives the direct decajoule-to-zeptojoule factor of 1e+22.

Simple example

1 daJ × 1e+22 = 1e+22 zJ

1 decajoule = 1e+22 zeptojoules.

Real-world example

100 daJ × 1e+22 = 1e+24 zJ

100 decajoules = 1e+24 zeptojoules.
