About Converting Decijoules to Decajoules

Decijoule and Decajoule both measure energy, and the conversion is especially useful for comparing nutrition labels (kilocalories) against scientific energy figures (joules). Specifically: Decijoule is 0.1 joule units, and decajoule is 10 joule units.

Formula

decajoules = decijoules × 0.01

This factor comes from each unit's defined relationship to the category's base unit: 1 decijoule equals 0.1 base units, and 1 decajoule equals 10 base units, so dividing one by the other gives the direct decijoule-to-decajoule factor of 0.01.

Simple example

1 dJ × 0.01 = 0.01 daJ

1 decijoule = 0.01 decajoules.

Real-world example

100 dJ × 0.01 = 1 daJ

100 decijoules = 1 decajoule.
