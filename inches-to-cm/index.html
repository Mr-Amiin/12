Frequently asked questions

How do you convert inch to centimeter?

Use the formula cm = in × 2.54. One inch equals 2.54 centimeters.

What is Inch?

The inch is an imperial unit of length used in the United States and the UK.

What is Centimeter?

The centimeter is one hundredth of a meter.

Is the inch to centimeter conversion exact?

Yes. Inch and Centimeter are both defined against internationally agreed constants, so the conversion factor used here is exact -- any rounding you see comes only from the number of decimal places displayed, not from the relationship itself.

How do I convert centimeter back to inch?

Use the reverse formula in = cm ÷ 2.54. This site's reverse converter does the same calculation automatically.

About this conversion

This length converter turns inch into centimeter using the exact defined relationship between the two units, not an approximation. A 12-inch ruler is about 30.48 cm long. The inch has roots in Roman and Anglo-Saxon measurement; it was standardized internationally in 1959 as exactly 2.54 centimeters. The centimeter is a metric subdivision of the meter introduced with the metric system in the 1790s. Because both units are built on internationally standardized constants, the same inch-to-centimeter factor works whether you're doing a quick everyday estimate or a calculation that needs to be precise.
