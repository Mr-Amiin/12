About Converting Square Attometers to Square Kilometers

Comparing Square Attometer and Square Kilometer is common in agriculture and real estate, where US land is typically measured in acres and most of the rest of the world uses hectares. Both are square metric area unit units.

Formula

square kilometers = square attometers × 1e-42

This factor comes from each unit's defined relationship to the category's base unit: 1 square attometer equals 1e-36 base units, and 1 square kilometer equals 1000000 base units, so dividing one by the other gives the direct square attometer-to-square kilometer factor of 1e-42.

Simple example

1 am2 × 1e-42 = 1e-42 km2

1 square attometer = 1e-42 square kilometers.

Understanding the Square Kilometer

Square Kilometer (km2) measures area. A square kilometer is one million square meters — the area of a 1 km × 1 km square. It is classified as a SI derived (metric prefix) unit.

Where it's used: Used worldwide for city, region, and country-scale area reporting.

Uses of the Square Kilometer today

  • National and regional land-area statistics
  • City and urban planning at large scale
  • Geography and cartography

History of the Square Kilometer

Like other metric-prefixed area units, the square kilometer follows automatically from the kilometer once the meter was standardized in the 1790s French metric reform.

Historical origin. Square Kilometer was not the invention of a single named person; it developed through the process described above.
