About Converting Square Millimeters to Square inches

Comparing Square Millimeter and Square inch is common in agriculture and real estate, where US land is typically measured in acres and most of the rest of the world uses hectares. Specifically: Square metric area unit, and a square inch is the area of a one-inch square.

Formula

square inches = square millimeters × 0.00155000310001

This factor comes from each unit's defined relationship to the category's base unit: 1 square millimeter equals 0.000001 base units, and 1 square inch equals 0.00064516 base units, so dividing one by the other gives the direct square millimeter-to-square inch factor of 0.00155000310001.

Simple example

1 mm2 × 0.0015500031 = 0.0015500031 in2

1 square millimeter = 0.0015500031 square inches.

Real-world example
