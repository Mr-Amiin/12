Understanding the Square foot

Square foot (ft2) measures area. A square foot is exactly 0.09290304 square meters, derived from the international foot fixed in 1959. It is classified as a US/imperial customary unit.

Where it's used: United States (real estate, construction) United Kingdom (some real estate and construction contexts)

Uses of the Square foot today

  • Real estate floor-space listings in the US
  • Construction, flooring, and materials estimation
  • Rental and commercial property pricing

History of the Square foot

The square foot follows directly from the foot, standardized in England over centuries and fixed at exactly 0.3048 meters by the 1959 International Yard and Pound Agreement. Squaring that value gives the modern square foot.

Historical origin. Square foot was not the invention of a single named person; it developed through the process described above.
