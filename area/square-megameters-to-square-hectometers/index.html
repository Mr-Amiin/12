About Converting Square Megameters to Square Hectometers

Comparing Square Megameter and Square Hectometer is common in agriculture and real estate, where US land is typically measured in acres and most of the rest of the world uses hectares. Both are square metric area unit units.

Formula

square hectometers = square megameters × 100000000

This factor comes from each unit's defined relationship to the category's base unit: 1 square megameter equals 1e+12 base units, and 1 square hectometer equals 10000 base units, so dividing one by the other gives the direct square megameter-to-square hectometer factor of 100000000.

Simple example

1 Mm2 × 100000000 = 100,000,000 hm2

1 square megameter = 100,000,000 square hectometers.

Real-world example

10 Mm2 × 100000000 = 1,000,000,000 hm2

10 square megameters = 1,000,000,000 square hectometers.
