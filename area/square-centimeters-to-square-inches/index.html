About Converting Square Centimeters to Square inches

Comparing Square Centimeter and Square inch is common in agriculture and real estate, where US land is typically measured in acres and most of the rest of the world uses hectares. Specifically: Square metric area unit, and a square inch is the area of a one-inch square.

Formula

square inches = square centimeters × 0.155000310001

This factor comes from each unit's defined relationship to the category's base unit: 1 square centimeter equals 0.0001 base units, and 1 square inch equals 0.00064516 base units, so dividing one by the other gives the direct square centimeter-to-square inch factor of 0.155000310001.

Simple example

1 cm2 × 0.15500031 = 0.15500031 in2

1 square centimeter = 0.15500031 square inches.

Real-world example

10 cm2 × 0.15500031 = 1.5500031 in2

10 square centimeters = 1.5500031 square inches.
