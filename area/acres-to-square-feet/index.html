About Converting Acres to Square feet

Comparing Acre and Square foot is common in agriculture and real estate, where US land is typically measured in acres and most of the rest of the world uses hectares. Acre is typically associated with united States, while Square foot is typically associated with united States (real estate, construction).

Formula

square feet = acres × 43560

This factor comes from each unit's defined relationship to the category's base unit: 1 acre equals 4046.8564224 base units, and 1 square foot equals 0.09290304 base units, so dividing one by the other gives the direct acre-to-square foot factor of 43560.

Simple example

1 ac × 43560 = 43,560 ft2

1 acre = 43,560 square feet.

Real-world example

10 ac × 43560 = 435,600 ft2

10 acres = 435,600 square feet.

Understanding the Acre

Acre (ac) measures area. The international acre is defined as exactly 4,046.8564224 square meters. It is classified as a US/imperial customary unit.

Where it's used: United States United Kingdom (informal/historical, land registry legacy) Countries with historical British land-survey systems

International acre vs. US survey acre. The US survey acre, based on the US survey foot, differs from the international acre by about two parts per million. The US retired the survey foot/acre for federal use at the end of 2022.

Uses of the Acre today

  • Measuring farmland, ranch, and rural property size in the US
  • Real estate listings and zoning for land parcels
  • Forestry and land-management reporting

History of the Acre

The acre originated in medieval England as a practical field measurement: the area a yoke of oxen could plow in one day, roughly a furlong (660 ft) long by a chain (66 ft) wide, giving the 43,560 square feet still used today.

Edward I's 1305 statute formalized this figure. In 1959, the same International Yard and Pound Agreement that fixed the pound also fixed the international acre at exactly 4,046.8564224 m², though the US separately retained a slightly different 'US survey acre' for some legacy land records until it was phased out in 2023.

Historical origin. Acre was not the invention of a single named person; it developed through the process described above.

Understanding the Square foot

Square foot (ft2) measures area. A square foot is exactly 0.09290304 square meters, derived from the international foot fixed in 1959. It is classified as a US/imperial customary unit.

Where it's used: United States (real estate, construction) United Kingdom (some real estate and construction contexts)

Uses of the Square foot today

  • Real estate floor-space listings in the US
  • Construction, flooring, and materials estimation
  • Rental and commercial property pricing

History of the Square foot

The square foot follows directly from the foot, standardized in England over centuries and fixed at exactly 0.3048 meters by the 1959 International Yard and Pound Agreement. Squaring that value gives the modern square foot.

Historical origin. Square foot was not the invention of a single named person; it developed through the process described above.
