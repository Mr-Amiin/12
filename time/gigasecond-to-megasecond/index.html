About Converting Gigaseconds to Megaseconds

Gigasecond and Megasecond are both everyday units of duration, and converting between them is one of the most routine calculations there is — scheduling, project planning, and billing all depend on it. Specifically: Gigasecond is 1000000000 second units, and megasecond is 1000000 second units.

Formula

megaseconds = gigaseconds × 1000

This factor comes from each unit's defined relationship to the category's base unit: 1 gigasecond equals 1000000000 base units, and 1 megasecond equals 1000000 base units, so dividing one by the other gives the direct gigasecond-to-megasecond factor of 1000.

Simple example

1 Gs × 1000 = 1,000 Ms

1 gigasecond = 1,000 megaseconds.

Real-world example

24 Gs × 1000 = 24,000 Ms

24 gigaseconds = 24,000 megaseconds.
