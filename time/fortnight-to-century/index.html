About Converting Fortnights to Centuries

Fortnight and Centuries are both everyday units of duration, and converting between them is one of the most routine calculations there is — scheduling, project planning, and billing all depend on it. Specifically: A fortnight is 14 days, and a century is 100 average Gregorian years.
