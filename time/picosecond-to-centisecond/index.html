About Converting Picoseconds to Centiseconds

Picosecond and Centisecond are both everyday units of duration, and converting between them is one of the most routine calculations there is — scheduling, project planning, and billing all depend on it. Specifically: Picosecond is 1e-12 second units, and centisecond is 0.01 second units.

Formula

centiseconds = picoseconds × 1e-10

This factor comes from each unit's defined relationship to the category's base unit: 1 picosecond equals 1e-12 base units, and 1 centisecond equals 0.01 base units, so dividing one by the other gives the direct picosecond-to-centisecond factor of 1e-10.

Simple example

1 ps × 1e-10 = 1e-10 cs

1 picosecond = 1e-10 centiseconds.

Real-world example

24 ps × 1e-10 = 2.4e-9 cs

24 picoseconds = 2.4e-9 centiseconds.
