Understanding the Hours

Hours (h) measures time. An hour is 60 minutes (3,600 seconds); dividing the day into 24 hours dates to ancient Egyptian timekeeping. It is classified as a Non-SI unit accepted for use with SI.

Where it's used: Universal

Uses of the Hours today

  • Work schedules and everyday timekeeping worldwide
  • Travel time and duration planning
  • Billing (hourly rates, parking, rentals)

History of the Hours

The 24-hour day traces to ancient Egypt, which divided daytime and nighttime into 12 parts each using sundials and star-based timekeeping; the modern fixed-length hour (rather than daylight-proportional 'temporal hours') became standard with the spread of mechanical clocks in medieval Europe.

Historical origin. Hours was not the invention of a single named person; it developed through the process described above.
