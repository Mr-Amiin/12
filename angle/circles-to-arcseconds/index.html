About Converting Circles to Arcseconds

Circle and Arcsecond are the two standard ways to measure angles — degrees for everyday and navigational use, radians for calculus and physics — so converting between them comes up constantly in math, engineering, and programming. Specifically: One complete circle, and one arcsecond is 1/3600 degree.

Formula

arcseconds = circles × 1296000

This factor comes from each unit's defined relationship to the category's base unit: 1 circle equals 6.28318530718 base units, and 1 arcsecond equals 0.0000048481368111 base units, so dividing one by the other gives the direct circle-to-arcsecond factor of 1296000.
