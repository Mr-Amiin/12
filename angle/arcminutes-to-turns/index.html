About Converting Arcminutes to Turns

Arcminute and Turn are the two standard ways to measure angles — degrees for everyday and navigational use, radians for calculus and physics — so converting between them comes up constantly in math, engineering, and programming. Specifically: One arcminute is 1/60 degree, and one full revolution.

Formula

turns = arcminutes × 0.0000462962962963

This factor comes from each unit's defined relationship to the category's base unit: 1 arcminute equals 0.000290888208666 base units, and 1 turn equals 6.28318530718 base units, so dividing one by the other gives the direct arcminute-to-turn factor of 0.0000462962962963.
