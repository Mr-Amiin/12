About Converting Milliradians to Turns

Milliradian and Turn are the two standard ways to measure angles — degrees for everyday and navigational use, radians for calculus and physics — so converting between them comes up constantly in math, engineering, and programming. Specifically: One milliradian is 0.001 radians, and one full revolution.

Formula

turns = milliradians × 0.000159154943092

This factor comes from each unit's defined relationship to the category's base unit: 1 milliradian equals 0.001 base units, and 1 turn equals 6.28318530718 base units, so dividing one by the other gives the direct milliradian-to-turn factor of 0.000159154943092.
