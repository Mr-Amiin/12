About Converting Meters per liter to Liters per kilometer

Meters per liter and Liters per kilometer are two of the ways the world measures fuel efficiency, and converting between them is essential for comparing a US car's window-sticker rating to a European or Asian one. Specifically: Meters per liter fuel economy unit, and liters per kilometer fuel economy unit.

Formula

Fuel economy formulas invert between distance-per-volume and volume-per-distance units as needed.

Efficiency-style units (like mpg or km/L) and consumption-style units (like L/100 km) measure the same underlying thing from opposite directions, so converting between the two families requires a reciprocal, not just a multiplication factor.

Simple example

1 m/L × 1000000000 = 1,000 L/km

1 meters per liter = 1,000 liters per kilometer.

Real-world example

30 m/L × 1000000000 = 33.33333333 L/km

30 meters per liter = 33.33333333 liters per kilometer.
