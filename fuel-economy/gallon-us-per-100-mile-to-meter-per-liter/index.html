About Converting US gallons per 100 miles to Meters per liter

US gallons per 100 miles and Meters per liter are two of the ways the world measures fuel efficiency, and converting between them is essential for comparing a US car's window-sticker rating to a European or Asian one. Specifically: US gallons per 100 miles fuel economy unit, and meters per liter fuel economy unit.

Formula

Fuel economy formulas invert between distance-per-volume and volume-per-distance units as needed.

Efficiency-style units (like mpg or km/L) and consumption-style units (like L/100 km) measure the same underlying thing from opposite directions, so converting between the two families requires a reciprocal, not just a multiplication factor.

Simple example
