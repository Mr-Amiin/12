About Converting Delisles to Newtons

Delisle and Newton are both historical temperature scales no longer in everyday use — this conversion is mainly useful for historical reference or comparing older scientific texts against each other.

Formula

Convert the source temperature to Kelvin, then convert Kelvin to the target scale.

Both scales are converted through Kelvin as a common absolute reference point.

Simple example

(0 − 32) × 5/9 = 33 °C

0 delisles = 33 newtons.

Frequently asked questions

How many newtons are in 1 delisle?

1 delisle equals 32.78 newtons, using the offset temperature formula rather than an estimate.

How do I convert delisle to newton?

Use the formula shown above: it involves both a multiplication and an addition/subtraction because the two scales don't share a common zero point.

How do I convert newton back to delisle?

Use the reverse factor: 1 newton equals 145.4545455 delisles. You can also use the swap control in the converter above to flip the direction instantly.

What is a delisle?

Delisle (De) is a unit of temperature.

What is a newton?

Is the Delisle to Newton conversion exact?

Yes. Both scales are formally defined relative to the Kelvin scale, so the conversion formula is exact — the only rounding involved is in how many decimal places you choose to display.
