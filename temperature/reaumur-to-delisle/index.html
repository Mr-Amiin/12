About Converting Reaumurs to Delisles

Reaumur and Delisle are both historical temperature scales no longer in everyday use — this conversion is mainly useful for historical reference or comparing older scientific texts against each other.

Formula

Convert the source temperature to Kelvin, then convert Kelvin to the target scale.

Both scales are converted through Kelvin as a common absolute reference point.

Simple example

(0 − 32) × 5/9 = 150 °C

0 reaumurs = 150 delisles.

Frequently asked questions

How many delisles are in 1 reaumur?

1 reaumur equals 148.125 delisles, using the offset temperature formula rather than an estimate.

How do I convert reaumur to delisle?

Use the formula shown above: it involves both a multiplication and an addition/subtraction because the two scales don't share a common zero point.

How do I convert delisle back to reaumur?

Use the reverse factor: 1 delisle equals 79.46666667 reaumurs. You can also use the swap control in the converter above to flip the direction instantly.

Is the Reaumur to Delisle conversion exact?

Yes. Both scales are formally defined relative to the Kelvin scale, so the conversion formula is exact — the only rounding involved is in how many decimal places you choose to display.
